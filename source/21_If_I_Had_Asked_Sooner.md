## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> Dear Daeun,
>
> It has been five years since our little café club closed, and I still think about it more often than I would like to admit. I am writing to tell you something I should have said long ago.
>
> If I had asked for help sooner, our café might still be open today. I remember how proud I was in those days. I believed that a good leader should solve every problem alone. So when our sales began to fall in the second semester, I told no one. I simply worked longer hours and hoped things would improve.
>
> They did not. If I had shared the numbers with you, you would have noticed what I missed. You were always better with people than I was. You would have talked to our regular customers and discovered why they had stopped coming. Instead, I kept the problem to myself until it was too late to fix.
>
> I sometimes wish I could go back to that October. If I were in that room again, I would put the sales chart on the table and say, "I don't know what to do. Can we figure this out together?" It sounds so easy now. At the time, it felt as if admitting a problem meant admitting that I had failed.
>
> Without that experience, however, I would not be the person I am today. These days, I lead a small team at work, and I ask for help all the time. When a project starts to go wrong, I tell the team immediately. Had I not learned that lesson with you, I would probably still be hiding problems and calling it strength.
>
> I also wish I had thanked you properly. You stayed late every Friday to clean the machines, and you never once complained. If you had not been there, the café would have closed in its first month, not its second year.
>
> If you are ever in the city, please let me know. I would love to buy you a coffee, and this time, I promise to ask how you are really doing.
>
> Your old partner, Minseo

## 문법 포인트

### 1. 가정법의 세 가지 기본형

가정법은 시제를 "한 칸 과거로" 당겨서 사실과 반대되는 상황을 표현합니다.

| 종류 | if절 | 주절 | 의미 | 이 글의 예 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 가정법 과거 | If + 과거 (be → were) | would/could/might + 동사원형 | 지금 사실의 반대 | **If I were** in that room again, I **would put** the sales chart on the table. |
| 가정법 과거완료 | If + had p.p. | would/could/might + have p.p. | 과거 사실의 반대 | **If I had shared** the numbers with you, you **would have noticed** what I missed. |
| 혼합 가정법 | If + had p.p. | would/might + 동사원형 (+ now/today) | 과거 조건 → 현재 결과 | **If I had asked** for help sooner, our café **might still be** open **today**. |

**혼합 가정법 읽는 법.** if절의 시점(과거)과 주절의 시점(today)이 다릅니다. "그때 ~했더라면, 지금 …할 텐데"로 해석합니다.

### 2. if 없이 가정을 나타내는 방법

| 형태 | 같은 뜻의 if절 | 이 글의 예 |
|---|---|---|
| Without + 명사 | If it were not for / If it had not been for | **Without that experience**, I would not be the person I am today. |
| if 생략 + 도치 | If I had not learned… | **Had I not learned** that lesson with you, I would probably still be hiding problems. |

if 생략 도치는 had, were, should가 if절에 있을 때만 가능하며, 격식 있는 글에서 자주 쓰입니다. 22편에서 도치를 더 자세히 다룹니다.

### 3. I wish + 가정법

| 형태 | 의미 | 이 글의 예 |
|---|---|---|
| I wish + 과거 | 지금 ~라면 좋을 텐데 | I sometimes **wish I could go back** to that October. |
| I wish + had p.p. | 그때 ~했더라면 좋았을 텐데 | I also **wish I had thanked** you properly. |

### 4. should have p.p. (과거에 대한 후회)

> I am writing to tell you something I **should have said** long ago.

"~했어야 했는데 (안 했다)"는 뜻입니다. 비슷한 형태로 could have p.p.(~할 수도 있었는데), might have p.p.(~했을지도 모른다)가 있습니다.

### 5. 가정법 vs 직설법 조건문

| 문장 | 종류 | 의미 |
|---|---|---|
| **If you are** ever in the city, please let me know. | 직설법 | 실제로 일어날 수 있는 일 |
| **If you had not been** there, the café would have closed. | 가정법 | 실제와 반대 (실제로는 거기 있었다) |

if가 있다고 모두 가정법이 아닙니다. 동사의 시제가 한 칸 과거로 당겨졌는지 확인하세요.
