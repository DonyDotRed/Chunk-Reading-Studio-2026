## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> The fable of a fox who waits for a perfect plan while a squirrel quietly fills her store captures a familiar pattern: high standards that, instead of improving work, prevent it from starting. In everyday speech, this pattern is often attributed to "perfectionism." The term, however, is used loosely, and its relationship to delay is more complex than the fable suggests. This essay analyzes the concept of perfectionism, distinguishes between its components, and considers which of them are most plausibly linked to procrastination.
>
> In psychological research, perfectionism is generally treated not as a single trait but as a cluster of related tendencies. A widely used distinction separates perfectionistic strivings from perfectionistic concerns. Strivings refer to setting high personal standards and working actively to meet them. Concerns refer to worry about making mistakes, fear of negative evaluation, and a persistent sense that one's performance is never adequate. The two components often occur together, yet they appear to be associated with quite different outcomes.
>
> Reviews of this literature suggest that perfectionistic strivings are frequently associated with neutral or even positive outcomes, such as persistence and engagement, at least when concerns are taken into account. Perfectionistic concerns, by contrast, tend to be associated with anxiety, avoidance, and lower well-being. If this distinction holds, then the problem in the fable is not that the fox held high standards. It is that he appears to have feared the consequences of falling short of them.
>
> This reading is consistent with research on procrastination. Large reviews have found the overall link between perfectionism and delay to be weaker than popular accounts assume. One plausible explanation is that broad measures of perfectionism combine the two components, so that the motivating effect of strivings partly offsets the inhibiting effect of concerns. Delay, on this view, is less a product of ambition than of the anticipated discomfort of producing something imperfect.
>
> The fable also illustrates a further mechanism: the substitution of planning for action. The fox's activity was not idle; he drew plans for walls, roofs, and protection from rain. Such planning may feel productive while postponing the moment at which work could be judged. In this sense, excessive preparation can function as a socially acceptable form of avoidance, one that is difficult to recognize precisely because it resembles diligence.
>
> These distinctions have practical implications. If the aim is to reduce delay, lowering one's standards may be unnecessary and even counterproductive. A more promising strategy may be to reduce the perceived cost of imperfection, for example by defining a first attempt explicitly as a draft, a prototype, or an experiment. A student who postpones a blog because her first post must be excellent might instead publish a short post labeled "Notes, version 1." The standard for later posts remains unchanged; what changes is the meaning attached to the first. The squirrel's remark that her small hole was "a place to start" can be read as precisely this kind of reframing: the first store was not expected to be the final one.
>
> In conclusion, perfectionism is better understood as a combination of high standards and fear of failure than as a single tendency. The evidence suggests that it is the latter that is most closely related to delay. The fable's lesson, therefore, is not that one should care less about quality, but that one should separate the pursuit of quality from the fear of being judged.

**① The fable of a fox who waits for a perfect plan while a squirrel quietly fills her store captures a familiar pattern: high standards that, instead of improving work, prevent it from starting.**

어휘: fable 우화 · capture 포착하다 · standard 기준 · prevent 막다

**② In everyday speech, this pattern is often attributed to "perfectionism."**
일상에서는 이 패턴을 흔히 "완벽주의" 탓으로 돌린다.
어휘: attribute A to B A의 원인을 B로 보다 · perfectionism 완벽주의

**③ The term, however, is used loosely, and its relationship to delay is more complex than the fable suggests.**
그러나 이 용어는 느슨하게 쓰이며, 미루기와의 관계도 우화가 보여 주는 것보다 복잡하다.
어휘: term 용어 · loosely 느슨하게 · delay 지연

**④ This essay analyzes the concept of perfectionism, distinguishes between its components, and considers which of them are most plausibly linked to procrastination.**
이 글은 완벽주의라는 개념을 분석하고 그 구성 요소를 구분한 뒤, 그중 어느 것이 미루기와 가장 그럴듯하게 연결되는지 고찰한다.
어휘: analyze 분석하다 · concept 개념 · component 구성 요소 · plausibly 그럴듯하게 · link 연결하다 · procrastination 미루기, 지연 행동

### 문단 2

**⑤ In psychological research, perfectionism is generally treated not as a single trait but as a cluster of related tendencies.**
심리학 연구에서 완벽주의는 일반적으로 단일한 특성이 아니라 서로 관련된 여러 경향의 묶음으로 다루어진다.
어휘: psychological 심리학의 · trait 특성 · cluster 묶음, 군집 · tendency 경향

**⑥ A widely used distinction separates perfectionistic strivings from perfectionistic concerns.**
널리 쓰이는 구분은 완벽주의적 추구와 완벽주의적 염려를 나누는 것이다.
어휘: distinction 구분 · separate A from B A와 B를 구분하다 · striving 노력, 추구 · concern 염려

**⑦ Strivings refer to setting high personal standards and working actively to meet them.**
추구란 높은 개인적 기준을 세우고 그것을 충족하려고 적극적으로 노력하는 것을 가리킨다.
어휘: refer to ~을 가리키다 · actively 적극적으로 · meet (기준을) 충족하다

**⑧ Concerns refer to worry about making mistakes, fear of negative evaluation, and a persistent sense that one's performance is never adequate.**
염려란 실수에 대한 걱정, 부정적 평가에 대한 두려움, 그리고 자기 성과가 결코 충분하지 않다는 끈질긴 느낌을 가리킨다.
어휘: evaluation 평가 · persistent 끈질긴 · performance 수행, 성과 · adequate 충분한

**⑨ The two components often occur together, yet they appear to be associated with quite different outcomes.**
두 요소는 흔히 함께 나타나지만, 상당히 다른 결과와 연관되는 것으로 보인다.
어휘: occur 나타나다 · be associated with ~와 연관되다 · outcome 결과

### 문단 3

**⑩ Reviews of this literature suggest that perfectionistic strivings are frequently associated with neutral or even positive outcomes, such as persistence and engagement, at least when concerns are taken into account.**
이 분야 문헌을 검토한 연구들에 따르면, 적어도 염려의 영향을 고려하고 나면 완벽주의적 추구는 끈기나 몰입처럼 중립적이거나 오히려 긍정적인 결과와 연관되는 경우가 많다.
어휘: review 검토 · literature 문헌 · neutral 중립적인 · persistence 끈기 · engagement 몰입, 참여 · take ~ into account ~을 고려하다

**⑪ Perfectionistic concerns, by contrast, tend to be associated with anxiety, avoidance, and lower well-being.**
반대로 완벽주의적 염려는 불안, 회피, 낮은 안녕감과 연관되는 경향이 있다.
어휘: anxiety 불안 · avoidance 회피 · well-being 안녕감, 행복

**⑫ If this distinction holds, then the problem in the fable is not that the fox held high standards.**
이 구분이 맞다면, 우화의 문제는 여우가 높은 기준을 가졌다는 데 있지 않다.
어휘: hold 유효하다

**⑬ It is that he appears to have feared the consequences of falling short of them.**
문제는 그가 그 기준에 미치지 못했을 때의 결과를 두려워했던 것으로 보인다는 데 있다.
어휘: to have p.p. (완료 부정사: 더 이전의 일) · consequence 결과 · fall short of ~에 미치지 못하다

### 문단 4

**⑭ This reading is consistent with research on procrastination.**
이런 해석은 미루기에 관한 연구와도 들어맞는다.
어휘: reading 해석, 독해 · be consistent with ~와 일치하다

**⑮ Large reviews have found the overall link between perfectionism and delay to be weaker than popular accounts assume.**
대규모 문헌 검토들은 완벽주의와 미루기의 전체적인 연관성이 대중적 설명이 가정하는 것보다 약하다는 사실을 발견했다.
어휘: overall 전체적인 · account 설명

**⑯ One plausible explanation is that broad measures of perfectionism combine the two components, so that the motivating effect of strivings partly offsets the inhibiting effect of concerns.**
그럴듯한 설명 하나는 완벽주의를 넓게 재는 척도가 두 요소를 한데 섞기 때문에, 추구의 동기 부여 효과가 염려의 억제 효과를 부분적으로 상쇄한다는 것이다.
어휘: plausible 그럴듯한 · measure 척도 · combine 결합하다 · motivating 동기를 부여하는 · offset 상쇄하다 · inhibiting 억제하는

**⑰ Delay, on this view, is less a product of ambition than of the anticipated discomfort of producing something imperfect.**
이 관점에서 보면, 미루기는 야망의 산물이라기보다 불완전한 결과물을 내놓을 때 예상되는 불편함의 산물이다.
어휘: on this view 이 견해에 따르면 · product 산물 · ambition 야망 · anticipated 예상되는 · discomfort 불편함 · imperfect 불완전한

### 문단 5

**⑱ The fable also illustrates a further mechanism: the substitution of planning for action.**
우화는 또 하나의 메커니즘도 보여 준다. 행동 대신 계획을 하는 것이다.
어휘: illustrate 예증하다 · mechanism 메커니즘, 작동 방식 · substitution 대체 · substitute A for B B 대신 A를 쓰다

**⑲ The fox's activity was not idle; he drew plans for walls, roofs, and protection from rain.**
여우가 빈둥거린 것은 아니었다. 벽과 지붕, 비를 막을 방법까지 계획을 그렸다.
어휘: idle 게으른, 놀고 있는 · protection 보호

**⑳ Such planning may feel productive while postponing the moment at which work could be judged.**
이런 계획은 생산적으로 느껴지지만, 그사이 일이 평가받을 수 있는 순간은 뒤로 밀린다.
어휘: productive 생산적인 · postpone 미루다 · judge 평가하다

**㉑ In this sense, excessive preparation can function as a socially acceptable form of avoidance, one that is difficult to recognize precisely because it resembles diligence.**
이런 의미에서 지나친 준비는 사회적으로 용인되는 회피의 한 형태로 작동할 수 있으며, 성실함과 닮았다는 바로 그 이유로 알아차리기 어렵다.
어휘: excessive 지나친 · preparation 준비 · function as ~으로 기능하다 · acceptable 용인되는 · one (= a form) 대용 · resemble 닮다 · diligence 성실함

### 문단 6

**㉒ These distinctions have practical implications.**
이런 구분은 실천적 함의를 지닌다.
어휘: implication 함의

**㉓ If the aim is to reduce delay, lowering one's standards may be unnecessary and even counterproductive.**
미루기를 줄이는 것이 목표라면, 기준을 낮추는 것은 불필요할 뿐 아니라 오히려 역효과를 낼 수 있다.
어휘: aim 목표 · counterproductive 역효과를 내는

**㉔ A more promising strategy may be to reduce the perceived cost of imperfection, for example by defining a first attempt explicitly as a draft, a prototype, or an experiment.**
더 유망한 전략은 불완전함의 체감 비용을 낮추는 것이다. 예를 들어 첫 시도를 초안, 시제품, 실험이라고 분명히 정해 두는 것이다.
어휘: promising 유망한 · perceived 인식된 · imperfection 불완전함 · explicitly 명시적으로 · draft 초안 · prototype 시제품, 원형

**㉕ A student who postpones a blog because her first post must be excellent might instead publish a short post labeled "Notes, version 1."**
첫 글이 훌륭해야 한다는 생각에 블로그를 미루는 학생이라면, 대신 "메모, 버전 1"이라는 제목으로 짧은 글을 올려 볼 수 있다.
어휘: publish 게시하다 · label 이름을 붙이다

**㉖ The standard for later posts remains unchanged; what changes is the meaning attached to the first.**
이후 글에 대한 기준은 그대로이고, 바뀌는 것은 첫 글에 부여하는 의미뿐이다.
어휘: unchanged 바뀌지 않은 · attach A to B A를 B에 붙이다

**㉗ The squirrel's remark that her small hole was "a place to start" can be read as precisely this kind of reframing: the first store was not expected to be the final one.**
자기의 작은 구멍이 "시작할 곳"이라는 다람쥐의 말은 바로 이런 관점 바꾸기로 읽을 수 있다. 첫 창고가 마지막 창고일 필요는 없었던 것이다.
어휘: remark 말 · reframing 재구성, 관점 바꾸기

### 문단 7

**㉘ In conclusion, perfectionism is better understood as a combination of high standards and fear of failure than as a single tendency.**
결론적으로 완벽주의는 단일한 경향이라기보다 높은 기준과 실패에 대한 두려움의 결합으로 이해하는 편이 낫다.
어휘: combination 결합

**㉙ The evidence suggests that it is the latter that is most closely related to delay.**
근거에 따르면 미루기와 가장 밀접하게 관련된 것은 후자이다.
어휘: it is ~ that (강조) · the latter 후자

**㉚ The fable's lesson, therefore, is not that one should care less about quality, but that one should separate the pursuit of quality from the fear of being judged.**
따라서 우화의 교훈은 품질에 덜 신경 쓰라는 것이 아니라, 품질을 추구하는 일과 평가받는 두려움을 분리하라는 것이다.
어휘: pursuit 추구
