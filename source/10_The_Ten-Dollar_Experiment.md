## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> Last spring, our economics teacher gave each team in our class exactly ten dollars. The rules were simple. We had two weeks to make as much money as possible. The team with the most profit would win, and all the money would go to a local animal shelter.
>
> My team had four members, and we had four different ideas. Jisoo wanted to sell cookies. Daniel wanted to wash cars. Hana suggested making phone cases, and I wanted to offer a homework-help service. Instead of arguing, we decided to test the cheapest version of each idea first.
>
> The cookies were the easiest to start. We spent three dollars on ingredients and sold twenty cookies for fifty cents each. That was ten dollars from three, but it took a lot of time. The car wash was harder. We used only one dollar for soap, but only two customers came, so we earned eight dollars.
>
> The phone cases were the most expensive idea. One case cost almost five dollars to make, and nobody wanted to pay more than six. The profit was too small. Homework help, on the other hand, cost nothing at all. Three younger students paid five dollars each for an hour of math help. It was the most profitable idea, but it was also the most tiring.
>
> After the first week, we compared our numbers. Homework help earned the most money per hour. Cookies were slower but more reliable. So in the second week, we did both. We baked cookies at night and sold them during our study sessions.
>
> At the end of the two weeks, our ten dollars had become one hundred and twelve dollars. We did not win. Another team earned a little more by selling flowers on Parents' Day. But we learned a more valuable lesson. The best idea is not always the one you like most. It is the one that the numbers support.

## 문법 포인트

### 1. 비교급·최상급 만드는 법

| 형용사 유형 | 비교급 | 최상급 | 이 글의 예 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1음절 | -er | the -est | hard → **harder**, slow → **slower**, young → **younger**, cheap → **the cheapest** |
| -y로 끝나는 2음절 | -ier | the -iest | easy → **the easiest** |
| 2음절 이상 | more ~ | the most ~ | **more reliable**, **more valuable**, **the most expensive**, **the most profitable**, **the most tiring** |
| 불규칙 | better / more | the best / the most | good → **the best**, much → **the most** money |

### 2. 비교 구문

| 구문 | 의미 | 이 글의 예 |
|---|---|---|
| 비교급 + than | ~보다 더 | nobody wanted to pay **more than** six |
| a little / much + 비교급 | 조금 / 훨씬 더 | earned **a little more** |
| as much ~ as possible | 가능한 한 많은 ~ | make **as much** money **as possible** |
| the + 최상급 + to부정사 | ~하기에 가장 …한 | **the easiest to start** |
| not always | 항상 ~인 것은 아니다 (부분 부정) | The best idea is **not always** the one you like most. |

### 3. 수량 표현

| 표현 | 의미 | 예 |
|---|---|---|
| exactly | 정확히 | exactly ten dollars |
| almost | 거의 | almost five dollars |
| only | 겨우, ~만 | only one dollar, only two customers |
| each | 각각, 개당 | for fifty cents each, paid five dollars each |
| per | ~당 | per hour |

**수치 검산 연습.** 쿠키는 3달러를 써서 10달러를 벌었으니 이익은 7달러, 이익률은 약 233%입니다. 세차는 1달러로 8달러를 벌어 이익 7달러, 숙제 도우미는 비용 0달러로 15달러를 벌었습니다. 낭독 후 각 아이디어의 이익을 영어로 비교하는 문장을 직접 만들어 보세요. 예: Homework help was **more profitable than** the car wash.
