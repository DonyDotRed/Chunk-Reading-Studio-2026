## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> By the time Mrs. Lee finally said yes, I had already heard her say no three times. She owned a small café near the train station, and I wanted her to sell my handmade notebooks next to her counter.
>
> The first time I visited, I had prepared a long speech. I had practiced it in front of my mirror for a week. But before I had finished my second sentence, she shook her head. "We don't sell things that aren't food," she said.
>
> Two weeks later, I went back. This time I had brought a sample notebook with the café's name printed on the cover. She looked at it carefully, but she said no again. She explained that she had tried selling postcards the year before and that nobody had bought them.
>
> After that, I almost gave up. I had spent most of my savings on paper and covers, and I had not sold a single notebook to any shop. My friend told me that I should find a different café. Instead, I asked myself a different question: what had Mrs. Lee actually said? She had not said my notebooks were bad. She had said that her customers had not bought postcards.
>
> So on my third visit, I offered something new. I suggested that she keep five notebooks for two weeks for free. If none sold, I would take them back. She thought about it for a long time, but in the end, she shook her head again. She said she did not have space on the counter.
>
> A month later, I visited one more time, just to say thank you for her time. To my surprise, she smiled and pointed to an empty corner of the counter. She had moved a coffee machine to make space. "I wanted to see if you would come back," she said. "People who come back four times usually make things that last."
>
> Since that day, she has sold over two hundred of my notebooks. I sometimes think about how close I came to quitting after the second no. If I had stopped then, I would never have learned that "no" sometimes means "not yet."

## 문법 포인트

### 1. 과거완료 = 과거보다 더 이전

과거완료(had + p.p.)는 이야기의 기준 시점이 이미 과거일 때, 그보다 **더 앞선 일**을 나타냅니다.

```
  더 이전 (had p.p.)          과거 기준 시점 (과거시제)            현재
  ──────●─────────────────────────●─────────────────────────────●──
  거울 앞에서 연습했다         처음 찾아갔다                      지금 회상
  (had practiced)              (visited)
```

| 쓰임 | 의미 | 이 글의 예 |
|---|---|---|
| 완료 | 기준 시점까지 이미 끝남 | By the time Mrs. Lee said yes, I **had** already **heard** her say no three times. |
| 경험 | 기준 시점 이전에 해 본 적 있음 | She explained that she **had tried** selling postcards. |
| 대과거 | 두 과거 사건의 선후 표시 | She **had moved** a coffee machine to make space. (가리키기 전에 이미 옮겨 둠) |
| 상태 | 기준 시점의 누적된 상태 | I **had spent** most of my savings. |

### 2. by the time + 과거, 주절은 과거완료

> **By the time** Mrs. Lee finally **said** yes, I **had** already **heard** her say no three times.

"~할 무렵에는 이미 …해 있었다"는 구조로, 과거완료를 가장 자연스럽게 끌어내는 표현입니다.

### 3. 과거완료가 꼭 필요하지 않은 경우

before, after처럼 순서가 분명한 접속사가 있으면 과거시제만 써도 됩니다.

- But before I **had finished** my second sentence, she shook her head. ✅
- But before I **finished** my second sentence, she shook her head. ✅ (의미 차이 거의 없음)

반대로, 순서를 알려 주는 말이 없으면 과거완료가 없을 때 의미가 달라집니다.

- She pointed to an empty corner. She **had moved** a coffee machine. → 옮긴 게 먼저
- She pointed to an empty corner. She **moved** a coffee machine. → 가리킨 뒤에 옮긴 것으로 읽힘

### 4. 부정문과 의문문

| 형태 | 구조 | 예 |
|---|---|---|
| 부정 | had not + p.p. | She **had not said** my notebooks were bad. |
| 의문 | (의문사) + had + 주어 + p.p.? | What **had** Mrs. Lee actually **said**? |

### 5. 미리보기: 가정법 과거완료

> If I **had stopped** then, I **would never have learned** that "no" sometimes means "not yet."

과거완료 형태가 if절에 들어가면 "그때 ~했더라면"이라는 과거 사실의 반대를 나타냅니다. 21편에서 본격적으로 다룹니다.
