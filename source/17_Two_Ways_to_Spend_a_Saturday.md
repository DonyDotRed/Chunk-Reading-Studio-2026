## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> Last Saturday, my two cousins, Minho and Yuna, both decided to work on their business ideas. Both of them wanted to sell handmade phone straps, and both had the same amount of money: thirty dollars. However, they spent their day in completely different ways.
>
> Minho stayed at home. He opened his laptop at nine in the morning and started writing a business plan. He researched prices, designed a logo, and built a spreadsheet that predicted his sales for the next three years. By evening, his plan was twenty pages long. Unlike Minho, Yuna did not write a plan at all.
>
> Instead, Yuna spent the morning making ten simple straps. Then she took them to a weekend flea market near the river. Whereas Minho's day was quiet and organized, Yuna's day was noisy and messy. She changed her price three times, moved her table twice, and talked to more than forty strangers.
>
> At the end of the day, the results were very different. Minho had a beautiful plan, but he had not spoken to a single customer. Yuna, on the other hand, had sold seven straps and collected a long list of comments. Several people had said the straps were too short. One woman asked whether she could order them in her school's colors.
>
> Similarly, both cousins felt tired that night, but for different reasons. Minho was tired because he had been thinking all day. In contrast, Yuna was tired because she had been doing all day.
>
> This does not mean that planning is useless. While Yuna's information was more real, Minho's spreadsheet could help her later when the business grew. The best approach probably combines both. Yet if you can choose only one way to spend your first Saturday, the market may teach you more than the laptop. A plan tells you what you hope will happen. A customer tells you what actually happens.

## 문법 포인트

### 1. 대조를 나타내는 말: 품사별로 구별하기

같은 "반면에"라는 뜻이라도 뒤에 무엇이 오는지, 문장을 어떻게 잇는지가 다릅니다.

| 품사 | 표현 | 뒤에 오는 것 | 이 글의 예 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 접속사 | while, whereas | 주어 + 동사 (한 문장 안에서 두 절 연결) | **Whereas** Minho's day was quiet and organized, Yuna's day was noisy and messy. |
| 접속부사 | however, on the other hand, in contrast, instead, yet | 콤마 후 새 문장 | **In contrast**, Yuna was tired because… |
| 전치사 | unlike | 명사 | **Unlike** Minho, Yuna did not write a plan. |

**구두점 주의.** however는 접속사가 아니므로 두 문장을 콤마 하나로 이을 수 없습니다.

- ❌ Minho stayed home, however Yuna went out.
- ✅ Minho stayed home. **However,** Yuna went out.
- ✅ Minho stayed home, **whereas** Yuna went out.

### 2. 유사를 나타내는 말

| 표현 | 이 글의 예 |
|---|---|
| both (둘 다) | **Both** of them wanted to sell handmade phone straps. |
| the same (같은) | **the same** amount of money |
| similarly (마찬가지로) | **Similarly**, both cousins felt tired that night. |

비교·대조문은 "같은 점 → 다른 점 → 결론" 순서로 짜는 것이 일반적입니다. 이 글은 첫 문단에서 공통점(both, the same)을 먼저 제시한 뒤, 둘째 문단부터 차이점을 다룹니다.

### 3. while의 두 가지 의미

| 의미 | 예 |
|---|---|
| ~하는 동안 (시간) | **While** I sat there in the heat, the ice melted. (01편) |
| ~인 반면 (대조) | **While** Yuna's information was more real, Minho's spreadsheet could help her later. |

두 절의 내용이 서로 맞서면 "반면", 같은 시간에 일어나면 "동안"으로 해석합니다.

### 4. 미리보기: 과거완료진행

> Minho was tired because he **had been thinking** all day.

과거의 어느 시점(그날 밤)까지 어떤 동작이 계속되고 있었음을 강조합니다. 과거완료는 18편에서 본격적으로 다룹니다.
