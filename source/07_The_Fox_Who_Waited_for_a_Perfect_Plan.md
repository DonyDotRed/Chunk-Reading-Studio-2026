## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> Once upon a time, a young fox lived at the edge of a large forest. Every winter, he was hungry, and every winter, he made the same promise. "Next spring, I will build a food store," he said. "I am going to fill it with nuts and berries."
>
> When spring came, the fox sat under a tree to think. He wanted to find the perfect place for his store. "It must be dry," he said to himself. "It must be close to water. "It must be hidden from the wolves." He spent many days looking for a place like that, but no place seemed good enough.
>
> Meanwhile, a small squirrel was running back and forth with nuts in her mouth. "What are you doing?" asked the fox. "I am trying to save food for winter," she answered. "Your hole is too small to hold much," said the fox. "And it is too close to the path." The squirrel only smiled. "It is a place to start," she said, and she went back to work.
>
> Summer passed, and the fox was still drawing plans in the dirt. He had a plan for the walls, a plan for the roof, and even a plan to keep out the rain. He had everything except food.
>
> Then the first snow fell. The fox ran to the squirrel's tree. To his surprise, her small hole was full. She had moved some of her nuts to a second hole and then to a third. "How did you do it?" he asked. "I did not wait to be ready," she said. "I got ready by doing it."
>
> The fox went to bed hungry that night. But the next morning, he got up early to collect his first nut.
>
> **Moral**
>
> A plan is useful only when it helps you to begin.

## 문법 포인트

### 1. to부정사의 세 가지 용법

to부정사(to + 동사원형)는 문장 안에서 명사, 형용사, 부사의 역할을 모두 할 수 있습니다. 해석은 위치를 보고 결정합니다.

| 용법 | 역할 | 해석 | 이 글의 예 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 명사적 | 목적어·주어·보어 | ~하는 것, ~하기 | He wanted **to find** the perfect place. |
| 형용사적 | 명사 뒤에서 수식 | ~할, ~하는 | It is a place **to start**. / a plan **to keep out** the rain |
| 부사적 (목적) | 동작의 목적 | ~하기 위해 | The fox sat under a tree **to think**. / He got up early **to collect** his first nut. |

**판별 요령.** 동사 바로 뒤(want, try, decide…)면 명사적, 명사 바로 뒤면 형용사적, 완전한 문장 뒤에 덧붙어 "왜?"에 답하면 부사적입니다.

### 2. too + 형용사 + to부정사

> Your hole is **too small to hold** much.

"너무 ~해서 …할 수 없다"는 뜻입니다. 11편에서 "too heavy to carry"로 다시 등장합니다. 부정어 없이도 부정의 의미가 들어 있다는 점에 주의하세요.

### 3. 미리보기: 직접화법의 형식

| 형식 | 예 | 비고 |
|---|---|---|
| "인용," + 주어 + 동사 | "It must be dry," **he said**. | 가장 흔한 형태 |
| "인용," + 동사 + 명사 주어 | "Your hole is too small," **said the fox**. | 주어가 명사일 때 도치 가능 |
| "인용?" + 주어 + asked | "How did you do it?" **he asked**. | 의문문은 물음표로 끝냄 |

주어가 대명사(he, she)일 때는 보통 도치하지 않습니다. said he는 옛 문체입니다. 인용문을 간접화법으로 바꾸는 법은 13편에서 다룹니다.
