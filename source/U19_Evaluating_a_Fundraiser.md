## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> **Purpose and scope**
>
> This report evaluates the student council's annual fundraising event, which was redesigned last year after the previous format produced disappointing results. The evaluation has two aims: to determine whether the redesign achieved its objectives, and to identify improvements for future events. It draws on attendance records, financial figures, and a short survey completed by students after the event. The report is intended for the council's planning committee and for teachers who supervise student activities.
>
> **Evaluation criteria**
>
> Three criteria were used. The first was financial effectiveness, defined as the amount raised for the library relative to the previous year. The second was participation, measured by attendance. The third was student satisfaction, assessed through a five-item survey distributed at the end of the event. These criteria were selected because they correspond directly to the problems identified before the redesign.
>
> **Findings**
>
> On all three criteria, the redesigned event performed substantially better than its predecessor. Attendance rose from approximately one hundred students to more than three hundred. Revenue increased from ninety dollars to four hundred and twenty, allowing the library to purchase thirty new books rather than six. Of the 150 students who completed the survey, approximately 80 percent rated the event positively, with the book swap and the online snack vote receiving the most favorable comments.
>
> Participation also became more representative. In the previous year, most attendees had been members of clubs that helped organize the bake sale. After the redesign, roughly half of the survey respondents reported that they had never attended a council event before, which suggests that the changes reached students who had previously been uninvolved.
>
> However, the findings also revealed weaknesses. Several respondents reported long queues at the snack table, suggesting that staffing was insufficient during the first hour. Others noted that the book swap was dominated by a small number of genres, which may have limited its appeal to some readers. In addition, because no data were collected on why students attended, it is not possible to determine which of the three changes contributed most to the improvement.
>
> **Conclusions**
>
> Overall, the evidence indicates that the redesign was successful. It should be noted, however, that the comparison relies on a single year before and after the change, and that factors unrelated to the redesign, such as the weather or competing events, may have influenced attendance. The improvement is therefore best interpreted as strong but not conclusive evidence that the changes were effective.
>
> **Recommendations**
>
> On the basis of these findings, three recommendations are made. First, it is recommended that the council increase the number of volunteers at food stations during the opening hour. Second, the council should actively invite contributions of underrepresented genres before the book swap, for example by announcing a theme. Third, it is recommended that a brief question be added to the post-event survey asking students what motivated them to attend, so that future evaluations can distinguish between the effects of individual changes.
>
> Finally, the council should consider maintaining the same core format for at least one further year. Repeating the event with only minor adjustments would make it possible to assess whether the improvement persists, providing a more reliable basis for long-term planning. It would also be advisable to record the number of books exchanged by genre, since this would allow the council to verify whether the second recommendation has been effective.

어휘: draw on ~을 활용하다 · attendance 참석 · figure 수치 · survey 설문 · complete 작성하다

**④ The report is intended for the council's planning committee and for teachers who supervise student activities.**
이 보고서는 학생회 기획 위원회와 학생 활동을 지도하는 교사들을 대상으로 한다.
어휘: be intended for ~을 위한 것이다 · committee 위원회 · supervise 감독하다

### 문단 2 · Evaluation criteria

**⑤ Three criteria were used.**
세 가지 기준을 사용했다.
어휘: criterion–criteria 기준

**⑥ The first was financial effectiveness, defined as the amount raised for the library relative to the previous year.**
첫째는 재정적 효과로, 전년도 대비 도서관을 위해 모은 금액으로 정의했다.
어휘: effectiveness 효과성 · define 정의하다 · raise (돈을) 모으다 · relative to ~에 비해

**⑦ The second was participation, measured by attendance.**
둘째는 참여로, 참석자 수로 측정했다.
어휘: participation 참여 · measure 측정하다

**⑧ The third was student satisfaction, assessed through a five-item survey distributed at the end of the event.**
셋째는 학생 만족도로, 행사가 끝날 때 배포한 5문항 설문으로 평가했다.
어휘: satisfaction 만족 · assess 평가하다 · item 문항 · distribute 배포하다

**⑨ These criteria were selected because they correspond directly to the problems identified before the redesign.**
이 기준들은 재설계 전에 확인된 문제들과 직접 대응하기 때문에 선택했다.
어휘: select 선택하다 · correspond to ~에 대응하다

### 문단 3 · Findings

**⑩ On all three criteria, the redesigned event performed substantially better than its predecessor.**
세 기준 모두에서 재설계된 행사는 이전 행사보다 상당히 좋은 성과를 냈다.
어휘: perform 성과를 내다 · substantially 상당히 · predecessor 이전 것, 전임자

**⑪ Attendance rose from approximately one hundred students to more than three hundred.**
참석자는 약 100명에서 300명 이상으로 늘었다.
어휘: approximately 대략

**⑫ Revenue increased from ninety dollars to four hundred and twenty, allowing the library to purchase thirty new books rather than six.**
수입은 90달러에서 420달러로 늘어, 도서관은 새 책을 6권이 아니라 30권 살 수 있었다.
어휘: revenue 수입 · purchase 구입하다

**⑬ Of the 150 students who completed the survey, approximately 80 percent rated the event positively, with the book swap and the online snack vote receiving the most favorable comments.**
설문에 응답한 150명 중 약 80퍼센트가 행사를 긍정적으로 평가했으며, 책 교환 행사와 온라인 간식 투표에 가장 호의적인 의견이 모였다.
어휘: rate 평가하다 · positively 긍정적으로 · swap 교환 · vote 투표 · favorable 호의적인 · comment 의견

### 문단 4

**⑭ Participation also became more representative.**
참여자 구성도 더 다양해졌다.
어휘: representative 대표성 있는

**⑮ In the previous year, most attendees had been members of clubs that helped organize the bake sale.**
전년도에는 참석자 대부분이 빵 바자회 준비를 도운 동아리 회원들이었다.
어휘: attendee 참석자 · organize 준비하다

**⑯ After the redesign, roughly half of the survey respondents reported that they had never attended a council event before, which suggests that the changes reached students who had previously been uninvolved.**
재설계 후에는 설문 응답자의 대략 절반이 학생회 행사에 처음 참석했다고 답했는데, 이는 변화가 이전에 참여하지 않던 학생들에게까지 닿았음을 시사한다.
어휘: roughly 대략 · reach 닿다 · uninvolved 관여하지 않는

### 문단 5

**⑰ However, the findings also revealed weaknesses.**
그러나 결과에서는 약점도 드러났다.
어휘: finding 결과 · reveal 드러내다 · weakness 약점

**⑱ Several respondents reported long queues at the snack table, suggesting that staffing was insufficient during the first hour.**
몇몇 응답자는 간식 테이블의 줄이 길었다고 답했는데, 이는 첫 한 시간 동안 인력이 부족했음을 시사한다.
어휘: respondent 응답자 · queue 줄 · staffing 인력 배치 · insufficient 불충분한

**⑲ Others noted that the book swap was dominated by a small number of genres, which may have limited its appeal to some readers.**
책 교환 행사에 소수 장르의 책이 지나치게 많았다는 지적도 있었는데, 이것이 일부 독자에게 행사의 매력을 떨어뜨렸을 수 있다.
어휘: note 언급하다 · dominate 지배하다 · genre 장르 · , which (앞 내용 전체) · appeal 매력

**⑳ In addition, because no data were collected on why students attended, it is not possible to determine which of the three changes contributed most to the improvement.**
또한 학생들이 왜 참석했는지에 관한 자료를 수집하지 않았기 때문에, 세 가지 변화 중 어느 것이 개선에 가장 크게 기여했는지는 판단할 수 없다.
어휘: collect 수집하다 · contribute 기여하다

### 문단 6 · Conclusions

**㉑ Overall, the evidence indicates that the redesign was successful.**
전반적으로 근거는 재설계가 성공적이었음을 보여 준다.
어휘: overall 전반적으로 · indicate 나타내다

**㉒ It should be noted, however, that the comparison relies on a single year before and after the change, and that factors unrelated to the redesign, such as the weather or competing events, may have influenced attendance.**
그러나 이 비교가 변화 전후 각각 한 해의 자료에 의존한다는 점, 그리고 날씨나 겹치는 다른 행사처럼 재설계와 무관한 요인이 참석에 영향을 주었을 수 있다는 점에 유의해야 한다.
어휘: rely on ~에 의존하다 · factor 요인 · unrelated 무관한 · competing 경쟁하는 · influence 영향을 미치다

**㉓ The improvement is therefore best interpreted as strong but not conclusive evidence that the changes were effective.**
따라서 이 개선은 변화가 효과적이었다는 강력하지만 결정적이지는 않은 근거로 해석하는 것이 가장 적절하다.
어휘: interpret 해석하다 · conclusive 결정적인 · effective 효과적인

### 문단 7 · Recommendations

**㉔ On the basis of these findings, three recommendations are made.**
이 결과를 바탕으로 세 가지 권고를 제시한다.
어휘: on the basis of ~에 근거하여 · recommendation 권고

**㉕ First, it is recommended that the council increase the number of volunteers at food stations during the opening hour.**
첫째, 학생회는 개장 첫 시간 동안 음식 부스의 자원봉사자 수를 늘릴 것을 권고한다.
어휘: recommend 권고하다 · that S + 동사원형 (가정법 현재) · volunteer 자원봉사자 · station 부스, 위치

**㉖ Second, the council should actively invite contributions of underrepresented genres before the book swap, for example by announcing a theme.**
둘째, 학생회는 책 교환 행사 전에 주제를 공지하는 등의 방법으로 부족한 장르의 책 기부를 적극적으로 요청해야 한다.
어휘: actively 적극적으로 · invite 청하다 · contribution 기부, 기여 · underrepresented 충분히 대표되지 않은 · announce 공지하다 · theme 주제

**㉗ Third, it is recommended that a brief question be added to the post-event survey asking students what motivated them to attend, so that future evaluations can distinguish between the effects of individual changes.**
셋째, 향후 평가에서 각 변화의 효과를 구분할 수 있도록, 행사 후 설문에 무엇 때문에 참석했는지 묻는 짧은 질문을 추가할 것을 권고한다.
어휘: be added (가정법 현재 수동) · post-event 행사 후의 · motivate 동기를 부여하다 · distinguish 구별하다 · individual 개별의

### 문단 8

**㉘ Finally, the council should consider maintaining the same core format for at least one further year.**
마지막으로, 학생회는 같은 핵심 형식을 적어도 한 해 더 유지하는 방안을 고려해야 한다.
어휘: maintain 유지하다 · core 핵심의 · further 추가의

**㉙ Repeating the event with only minor adjustments would make it possible to assess whether the improvement persists, providing a more reliable basis for long-term planning.**
약간만 조정한 채 행사를 한 번 더 열면 개선이 지속되는지 평가할 수 있어, 장기 계획을 위한 더 신뢰할 만한 근거를 얻게 될 것이다.
어휘: minor 사소한 · adjustment 조정 · make it possible to ~을 가능하게 하다 · assess 평가하다 · persist 지속되다 · reliable 신뢰할 만한 · basis 근거 · long-term 장기적인

**㉚ It would also be advisable to record the number of books exchanged by genre, since this would allow the council to verify whether the second recommendation has been effective.**
장르별로 교환된 책의 수를 기록해 두는 것도 바람직하다. 그래야 두 번째 권고가 효과가 있었는지 확인할 수 있다.
어휘: advisable 바람직한 · verify 확인하다

## 학술 영어 포인트

### 1. 평가 보고서의 네 층위

평가 보고서에서는 **사실, 판단, 행동**을 명확히 분리합니다. 이 구분이 흐려지면 보고서의 신뢰도가 떨어집니다.

| 절 | 담는 것 | 동사·표현의 특징 | 이 글의 예 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evaluation criteria | 무엇으로 평가했는가 | 과거 수동태, 정의 | Three criteria **were used**… **defined as**… **measured by**… |
| Findings | 관찰된 사실 | 수치, 과거시제 | Attendance **rose from** approximately one hundred **to** more than three hundred. |
| Conclusions | 사실에 근거한 판단 | indicate, 해석 수준 조정 | the evidence **indicates** that… **best interpreted as strong but not conclusive evidence** |
| Recommendations | 앞으로 할 행동 | recommend that + 동사원형, should | **it is recommended that** the council **increase**… |

### 2. 권고의 가정법 현재 (mandative subjunctive)

recommend, suggest, insist, require 뒤의 that절에서는 주어의 인칭·수와 관계없이 **동사원형**을 씁니다.

| 문장 | 동사 형태 |
|---|---|
| it is recommended that the council **increase** the number of volunteers | increase (increases ❌) |
| it is recommended that a brief question **be** added | be added (is added ❌) |

영국 영어에서는 should를 넣어 "that the council **should** increase…"로 쓰기도 합니다. 18편의 "I suggested that she **keep** five notebooks"도 같은 구조였습니다.

### 3. 전후 비교 설계의 한계

| 한계 | 이 글의 표현 |
|---|---|
| 측정 시점이 하나뿐 | the comparison relies on **a single year before and after the change** |
| 혼재 요인 (confounding factors) | factors **unrelated to the redesign**, such as the weather or competing events, **may have influenced** attendance |
| 개별 효과 분리 불가 | **it is not possible to determine which of the three changes contributed most** |
| 해석 수준 조정 | **strong but not conclusive evidence** |

마지막 권고("같은 형식을 한 해 더 유지하라")는 바로 이 한계를 보완하기 위한 것입니다. 좋은 평가 보고서는 **평가의 한계를 다음 평가의 설계로 연결**합니다.

### 4. 원작 19편과의 연결

| 19편 사례 보고문 | U19 평가 보고서 |
|---|---|
| the usual bake sale earned only ninety dollars because fewer students came | (Purpose) after the previous format produced disappointing results |
| More than three hundred students attended, which was three times the number of the previous year. | (Findings) Attendance rose from approximately one hundred students to more than three hundred. |
| The library was therefore able to buy thirty new books. | allowing the library to purchase thirty new books rather than six |
| Because the council asked before acting, it solved the real problem… | (Conclusions) strong but not conclusive evidence that the changes were effective |
| — | (Recommendations) 19편에는 없던, 다음 행동을 위한 절 |

19편이 "무엇이 일어났고 무엇을 배웠는가"를 보고했다면, U19는 **기준을 세워 판단하고 다음 행동을 권고**합니다.
