## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> **Jun:** Have you thought about what you want to do after college?
>
> **Sora:** I think I want a stable job at a big company. A regular salary sounds safe to me.
>
> **Jun:** I see your point, but I want to start my own business someday.
>
> **Sora:** Really? Isn't that risky? Most new businesses fail in the first few years.
>
> **Jun:** That's true, but a job isn't completely safe either. Companies cut workers all the time.
>
> **Sora:** Fair enough. Still, with a salary, I know how much money I will get every month. I can plan my life.
>
> **Jun:** I agree that planning is important. But in my opinion, freedom matters more. I want to decide how I spend my time.
>
> **Sora:** I'm not sure about that. Business owners often work longer hours than employees.
>
> **Jun:** You may be right. My uncle runs a restaurant, and he works almost every weekend.
>
> **Sora:** Exactly. So why do you still want it?
>
> **Jun:** Because he loves his work. He says he would never go back to an office.
>
> **Sora:** Hmm. I guess it depends on the person.
>
> **Jun:** I think so too. Actually, maybe we don't have to choose only one. I could work at a company first and learn the basics.
>
> **Sora:** And I could start a small project on weekends. That way, I can test my ideas without losing my salary.
>
> **Jun:** That sounds like a good plan for both of us.
>
> **Sora:** Agreed. Let's talk again in ten years and see who was right.

## 문법 포인트

### 1. 토론 표현 지도

| 기능 | 표현 | 강도·뉘앙스 |
|---|---|---|
| 의견 제시 | I think… / In my opinion, … / I guess… | guess가 가장 조심스러움 |
| 완전 동의 | Exactly. / I think so too. / Agreed. | 짧을수록 확고함 |
| 조건부 동의 | Fair enough. / That's true. / I agree that… | 상대 논리를 인정 |
| 인정 후 반박 | I see your point, but… / That's true, but… | 대화를 이어 가며 반대 |
| 부드러운 반대 | I'm not sure about that. / You may be right, but… | 직접 "No"를 피함 |
| 타협 | It depends on… / That way, … | 결론을 열어 둠 |

이 대화는 "주장 → 인정 → 반박 → 사례 → 타협"의 흐름을 따릅니다. 실제 영어 토론과 논증 글쓰기(23편)의 기본 구조이기도 합니다.

### 2. too vs either

| 문장 | 해석 |
|---|---|
| I think so **too**. | 나도 그렇게 생각해. (긍정문) |
| A job isn't completely safe **either**. | 직장도 완전히 안전하지는 **않아**. (부정문) |

"~도"라는 뜻이지만, 긍정문에는 too, 부정문에는 either를 씁니다.

### 3. 미리보기: 부정 의문문과 대답

> **Isn't** that risky? 그거 위험하지 않아?

영어는 질문 형태와 상관없이 **내용이 긍정이면 Yes, 부정이면 No**로 답합니다.

| 대답 | 의미 |
|---|---|
| **Yes**, it is. | 응, 위험해. |
| **No**, it isn't. | 아니, 위험하지 않아. |

한국어 "응/아니"와 반대로 느껴질 수 있으니 주의하세요.
