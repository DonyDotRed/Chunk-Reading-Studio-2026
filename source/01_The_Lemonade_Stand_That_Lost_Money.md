## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> When I was fifteen, I decided to start my first business. It was a hot summer, and I wanted to earn some money for a new bicycle. After I thought about it for a few days, I chose a simple idea: a lemonade stand.
>
> I spent almost all of my savings before I sold a single cup. I bought fresh lemons, a large glass jar, and colorful paper cups. I even paid a friend to paint a sign for me. I believed that everything had to be perfect. As soon as the stand was ready, I put it at the end of our quiet street and waited.
>
> I waited for a long time. Only three cars passed by that morning, and none of them stopped. While I sat there in the heat, the ice in my jar slowly melted. By noon, my lemonade was warm, and I was tired. I did not sell anything until a neighbor walked over and bought one cup, mostly because she felt sorry for me.
>
> At the end of the week, I counted my money. I had spent forty dollars, but I had earned only nine. I felt embarrassed, and I wanted to forget the whole thing.
>
> Now that I am older, I understand that the stand did not fail because lemonade was a bad idea. It failed because I made three mistakes. First, I spent too much money before I knew if anyone wanted my product. Second, I chose a place where there were no customers. Third, I never asked people what they actually wanted.
>
> Now, whenever I start something new, I remember that summer. Before I spend money, I test the idea in a small way. I go where the people are, and I ask questions until I understand their needs. The lemonade stand lost money, but it taught me lessons that were worth much more than nine dollars.

| 접속사 | 의미 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| when | ~할 때 | **When** I was fifteen, I decided… ① |
| after | ~한 후에 | **After** I thought about it… ③ |
| before | ~하기 전에 | …**before** I sold a single cup. ④ |
| as soon as | ~하자마자 | **As soon as** the stand was ready… ⑧ |
| while | ~하는 동안 | **While** I sat there in the heat… ⑪ |
| until | ~할 때까지 | I did not sell anything **until**… ⑬ |
| whenever | ~할 때마다 | **whenever** I start something new… ㉒ |

`not A until B` 구문(⑬)은 "B하고 나서야 비로소 A했다"로 옮기면 자연스럽습니다.

### 3. 미리보기: 과거완료 (had + p.p.)

> I **had spent** forty dollars, but I **had earned** only nine. ⑮

돈을 센 시점(과거)보다 돈을 쓰고 번 일이 더 먼저 일어났기 때문에 과거완료를 씁니다. 18편에서 자세히 다룹니다.
