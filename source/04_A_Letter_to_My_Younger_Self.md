## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> Dear fifteen-year-old me,
>
> I am writing this letter from ten years in the future. I know you cannot read it, but I want to tell you a few things anyway.
>
> First, you should ask more questions. Right now, you think questions make you look weak. You might believe that smart people already know everything. They don't. The smartest people I know ask questions all the time.
>
> Second, you can say no. You do not have to join every club or please every friend. Your time is limited, and you must protect it for the things that matter to you.
>
> Third, you could start that small project today. You keep saying, "Maybe next year." But next year will have its own excuses. You do not need a perfect plan. You only need a first step.
>
> Fourth, you might fail, and that is fine. Some of your ideas will not work. People may laugh at one or two of them. However, every failure will teach you something that success cannot.
>
> Fifth, you should save a little money every month. It will not look like much at first. But one day, you might need it for an idea that you cannot stop thinking about. When that day comes, you will thank me.
>
> Finally, you should be kinder to yourself. You will make mistakes, and you will feel embarrassed sometimes. Those feelings will pass. What will stay is the courage you build by trying again.
>
> I cannot change your past, but you can still shape your future. Please start now.
>
> With love, your older self

## 문법 포인트

### 1. 조동사의 의미 지도

조동사 뒤에는 항상 동사원형이 옵니다. 같은 조동사도 문맥에 따라 의미가 달라집니다.

| 조동사 | 주요 의미 | 강도 | 이 글의 예 |
|---|---|---|---|
| must | 강한 의무 (~해야 한다) | 강 | you **must protect** it |
| should | 충고 (~하는 게 좋다) | 중 | you **should ask** more questions |
| have to | 외부 사정에 의한 의무 | 강 | (부정형만 등장) |
| can | 능력·허락 (~할 수 있다, ~해도 된다) | — | you **can say** no |
| could | 가능성·부드러운 제안 | 약 | you **could start** that project today |
| will | 미래·예측 (~할 것이다) | 확신 높음 | Some ideas **will not work**. |
| may | 추측 (~일지도 모른다) | 중간 | People **may laugh**. |
| might | 약한 추측 | may보다 약간 약함 | You **might fail**. |

### 2. 꼭 구별할 쌍: must not vs don't have to

| 표현 | 의미 | 예 |
|---|---|---|
| must not | ~하면 **안 된다** (금지) | You **must not** give up. 포기하면 안 돼. |
| don't have to | ~할 **필요 없다** (불필요) | You **don't have to** join every club. 모든 동아리에 들 필요는 없어. |

긍정문에서는 must와 have to가 비슷하지만, 부정문에서는 의미가 완전히 달라집니다.

### 3. 편지 형식

| 위치 | 표현 | 의미 |
|---|---|---|
| 첫머리 | Dear + 이름, | ~에게 |
| 끝맺음 | With love, / Best wishes, / Sincerely, | 사랑을 담아 / 행운을 빌며 / 진심으로 |
| 서명 | 보내는 사람 | |
