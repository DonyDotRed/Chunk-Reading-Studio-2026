## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> **Background**
>
> Every year, our student council raises money for new books for the school library. Last year, however, the usual bake sale earned only ninety dollars because fewer students came. As a result, the library could buy only six new books. The council decided that it needed a different approach.
>
> **Problem**
>
> To understand the problem, the council surveyed one hundred students. The answers were clear. Many students skipped the bake sale due to the timing; it was held during lunch, when most students wanted to rest. Others said the food was always the same. Since nothing ever changed, there was little reason to come.
>
> **Action**
>
> Based on these results, the council made three changes. First, it moved the event to the end of the school day. Second, it replaced the bake sale with a "book swap," where students could trade used books for a small fee. Third, it asked students to vote online for the snacks that would be sold. Thanks to the vote, students felt that the event belonged to them.
>
> **Results**
>
> The changes led to a big difference. More than three hundred students attended, which was three times the number of the previous year. Consequently, the event earned four hundred and twenty dollars. In addition, about two hundred used books were exchanged, so many students went home with something new to read. The library was therefore able to buy thirty new books.
>
> **Lessons**
>
> The most important lesson was not about books or snacks. The council had assumed that students were not interested in the library. In fact, the low attendance was caused by poor timing and a lack of choice. Because the council asked before acting, it solved the real problem instead of the imagined one. Asking questions costs almost nothing, but it can result in much better decisions.

## 문법 포인트

### 1. 원인을 나타내는 말

| 품사 | 표현 | 뒤에 오는 것 | 이 글의 예 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 접속사 | because, since, as | 주어 + 동사 | …**because** fewer students came. / **Since** nothing ever changed, … |
| 전치사(구) | because of, due to, thanks to | 명사 | **due to** the timing / **Thanks to** the vote, … |

**thanks to**는 좋은 결과의 원인에, **due to**는 중립적이거나 나쁜 결과의 원인에 주로 씁니다.

### 2. 결과를 나타내는 말

| 품사 | 표현 | 이 글의 예 |
|---|---|---|
| 접속사 | so | …were exchanged, **so** many students went home with something new. |
| 접속부사 | as a result, consequently, therefore | **As a result**, the library could buy only six new books. / **Consequently**, the event earned $420. |
| 문중 접속부사 | therefore (주어 뒤·동사 사이) | The library was **therefore** able to buy thirty new books. |

### 3. 인과 관계를 나타내는 동사: 방향에 주의

| 동사 | 방향 | 예 |
|---|---|---|
| A lead to B | A(원인) → B(결과) | The changes **led to** a big difference. |
| A result in B | A(원인) → B(결과) | Asking questions can **result in** better decisions. |
| B result from A | B(결과) ← A(원인) | Better decisions can **result from** asking questions. |
| B be caused by A | B(결과) ← A(원인) | The low attendance **was caused by** poor timing. |

result in과 result from은 전치사 하나 차이로 방향이 정반대입니다. 수능 어휘 문제에서 자주 묻는 쌍입니다.

### 4. 보고문의 구조

이 글은 실제 사례 보고서의 전형적인 5단 구조(Background → Problem → Action → Results → Lessons)를 따릅니다. 각 단계에서 쓰이는 연결어가 다릅니다.

| 단계 | 주로 쓰이는 연결어 |
|---|---|
| Background | however, as a result |
| Problem | due to, since |
| Action | first, second, third, thanks to |
| Results | lead to, consequently, in addition, therefore |
| Lessons | in fact, because, result in |

### 5. 미리보기: 앞 문장 전체를 받는 , which

> More than three hundred students attended**, which** was three times the number of the previous year.

여기서 which는 특정 명사가 아니라 "300명 넘게 참석했다"는 앞 내용 전체를 받습니다. 이 용법에는 that을 쓸 수 없습니다.
