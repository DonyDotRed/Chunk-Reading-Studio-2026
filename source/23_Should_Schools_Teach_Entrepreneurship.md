## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> In recent years, a growing number of schools have begun offering classes in entrepreneurship, the skill of starting and running a business. Supporters believe these classes prepare students for an uncertain future. Critics, however, argue that schools should focus on basic subjects. Although both sides make reasonable points, I believe that entrepreneurship deserves a place in the classroom, as long as it is taught in the right way.
>
> Admittedly, the critics have a fair concern. School time is limited, and many students still struggle with reading, writing, and mathematics. Even if entrepreneurship is useful, it should not replace these foundations. Furthermore, not every student wants to start a business, and some may feel pressured to become "founders" when they would rather become teachers, nurses, or scientists.
>
> Despite these concerns, there are strong reasons to teach it. First, entrepreneurship is not only about business. It teaches students how to identify problems, test ideas, work in teams, and recover from failure. These skills are valuable in almost any career. A nurse who notices a weakness in a hospital process uses the same thinking as a founder who notices a gap in the market.
>
> Second, entrepreneurship classes can make other subjects more meaningful. Though many students find percentages boring, they suddenly care about them when calculating the profit of their own small project. In spite of its reputation as a "soft" subject, a well-designed course can strengthen mathematics, writing, and communication at the same time.
>
> Third, these classes may reduce inequality. Students from families that own businesses often learn these skills at home. Others never get the chance. Even though a single course cannot solve this problem, it can give every student at least a basic understanding of how ideas become real.
>
> Nevertheless, the way the subject is taught matters a great deal. Entrepreneurship should not be presented as a path to quick wealth, nor should success be measured only by money. Instead, the focus should be on curiosity, problem-solving, and learning from mistakes. Projects could include helping a local charity or improving something within the school itself.
>
> In conclusion, while the critics are right to protect basic subjects, entrepreneurship can support those subjects rather than compete with them. Taught with care, it does not turn every student into a business owner. It turns them into people who believe that problems can be solved, and that is a lesson worth teaching.

수업 하나로 이 문제를 해결할 수는 없지만, 적어도 모든 학생이 아이디어가 어떻게 현실이 되는지 기본적으로 이해하게 할 수는 있다.
어휘: even though (사실 인정) 비록 ~이지만 · at least 적어도

### 문단 6

**㉑ Nevertheless, the way the subject is taught matters a great deal.**
그렇더라도, 이 과목을 어떻게 가르치느냐가 매우 중요하다.
어휘: nevertheless 그럼에도 불구하고 · a great deal 매우, 많이

**㉒ Entrepreneurship should not be presented as a path to quick wealth, nor should success be measured only by money.**
창업 교육을 빨리 부자가 되는 길로 소개해서는 안 되며, 성공을 돈으로만 재서도 안 된다.
어휘: present 제시하다 · path 길 · wealth 부 · nor + 조동사 + S (부정 도치) · measure 측정하다

**㉓ Instead, the focus should be on curiosity, problem-solving, and learning from mistakes.**
대신 호기심, 문제 해결, 실수에서 배우는 태도에 초점을 맞춰야 한다.
어휘: focus 초점 · curiosity 호기심

**㉔ Projects could include helping a local charity or improving something within the school itself.**
프로젝트로는 지역 자선 단체를 돕거나 학교 안의 무언가를 개선하는 일을 할 수 있다.
어휘: include 포함하다 · charity 자선 단체 · improve 개선하다 · within ~안의

### 문단 7

**㉕ In conclusion, while the critics are right to protect basic subjects, entrepreneurship can support those subjects rather than compete with them.**
결론적으로, 기본 과목을 지키려는 비판자들의 주장도 옳지만, 창업 교육은 그 과목들과 경쟁하기보다 오히려 뒷받침할 수 있다.
어휘: in conclusion 결론적으로 · while (양보) ~이긴 하지만 · protect 지키다 · support 뒷받침하다 · rather than ~하기보다 · compete 경쟁하다

**㉖ Taught with care, it does not turn every student into a business owner.**
신중하게 가르친다면, 창업 교육은 모든 학생을 사업가로 만들지 않는다.
어휘: (If it is) Taught (분사구문·조건) · turn A into B A를 B로 바꾸다

**㉗ It turns them into people who believe that problems can be solved, and that is a lesson worth teaching.**
대신 문제는 해결될 수 있다고 믿는 사람으로 만들어 준다. 그리고 그것이야말로 가르칠 가치가 있는 교훈이다.
어휘: worth -ing ~할 가치가 있는

## 문법 포인트

### 1. 양보 표현: 품사별 정리

양보란 "상대 주장이나 불리한 사실을 일단 인정한 뒤 내 주장을 펴는 것"입니다. 논증문의 핵심 장치입니다.

| 품사 | 표현 | 뒤에 오는 것 | 이 글의 예 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 접속사 | although, though, even though | 주어 + 동사 | **Although** both sides make reasonable points, I believe… |
| 접속사 | even if | 주어 + 동사 (가정) | **Even if** entrepreneurship is useful, it should not replace… |
| 접속사 | while | 주어 + 동사 | **while** the critics are right to protect basic subjects, … |
| 전치사 | despite, in spite of | 명사(구) | **Despite** these concerns, … / **In spite of** its reputation… |
| 부사 | admittedly, nevertheless | 콤마 후 문장 | **Admittedly**, the critics have a fair concern. / **Nevertheless**, the way… matters. |

**흔한 오류.** despite 뒤에 절을 쓰면 안 됩니다.
- ❌ **Despite** the critics are right, …
- ✅ **Although** the critics are right, …
- ✅ **Despite** the fact that the critics are right, …

### 2. even though vs even if

| 표현 | 전제 | 예 |
|---|---|---|
| even though | 사실임을 인정 | **Even though** a single course cannot solve this problem, … (실제로 해결 못 함) |
| even if | 가정 (사실일 수도, 아닐 수도) | **Even if** entrepreneurship is useful, … (유용하다고 치더라도) |

### 3. 찬반 논증문의 구조

| 단계 | 기능 | 이 글의 표지어 |
|---|---|---|
| 서론 | 쟁점 소개 + 내 입장 | Supporters believe… Critics argue… Although…, I believe… |
| 반론 인정 | 상대 논거를 공정하게 제시 | Admittedly, … Even if… Furthermore, … |
| 반박 + 근거 | 내 논거 1, 2, 3 | Despite these concerns, … First, … Second, … Third, … |
| 조건 제시 | 내 주장의 한계와 조건 | Nevertheless, … should not… nor… Instead, … |
| 결론 | 양측 정리 + 최종 입장 | In conclusion, while…, … |

반론을 먼저 인정하면 글쓴이가 공정하다는 인상을 주고, 이어지는 반박이 더 설득력을 얻습니다. 06편 대화문의 "I see your point, but…"을 글쓰기로 옮긴 것이라고 볼 수 있습니다.

### 4. 복습: 분사구문의 조건 의미

> **Taught with care**, it does not turn every student into a business owner.
> = **If it is taught** with care, …

15편에서 배운 분사구문이 여기서는 "~한다면"이라는 조건의 의미로 쓰였습니다.
