## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> Standing outside the classroom door, I could hear my own heartbeat. Inside, five judges were waiting to hear my business idea. My hands were cold, and my notes, folded too many times, were starting to tear. I seriously thought about walking away.
>
> Three months earlier, I had entered a student startup contest. Feeling confident, I had told everyone about it. My idea was a simple app connecting students who wanted to share textbooks. I believed in the idea, but I had never presented anything to strangers.
>
> Preparing for the contest, I practiced every night. Watching myself in the mirror, I noticed bad habits. I touched my hair too often. I spoke too fast when I was nervous. Recording my voice on my phone, I learned to slow down and pause after important points.
>
> Still, practice did not remove the fear. Lying in bed the night before, I imagined every possible disaster. What if I forgot my words? What if the judges laughed? Unable to sleep, I finally got up and wrote one sentence on a card: "You do not need to feel ready. You only need to walk in."
>
> So, holding that card in my pocket, I opened the door. The first minute was terrible. My voice shook, and I skipped a slide. But something strange happened after that. Focused on explaining my idea, I slowly forgot about myself. One judge nodded. Another wrote something down. When I finished, they asked me four questions, and I answered all of them.
>
> I did not win first prize. I came in third. Walking home that evening, however, I felt like a winner. I had not beaten the other teams. I had beaten the voice in my head that told me to stay outside.
>
> Since then, I have learned that fear rarely disappears before we act. More often, it disappears while we are acting. Waiting for courage, many people never begin. Walking in anyway, a few discover that courage was waiting for them inside the room.

## 문법 포인트

### 1. 분사구문 만드는 3단계

부사절(접속사 + 주어 + 동사)을 짧게 줄인 것이 분사구문입니다.

| 단계 | 할 일 | 예 |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 원래 문장 | **While I was preparing** for the contest, I practiced every night. |
| 1 | 접속사 생략 | ~~While~~ I was preparing for the contest, … |
| 2 | 주절과 같은 주어 생략 | ~~I~~ was preparing for the contest, … |
| 3 | 동사를 -ing로 (be동사는 Being → 생략 가능) | **Preparing** for the contest, I practiced every night. |

### 2. 분사구문의 의미

접속사가 사라졌으므로, 의미는 문맥으로 판단합니다.

| 의미 | 복원 접속사 | 이 글의 예 |
|---|---|---|
| 때 | when, while | **Lying** in bed the night before, I imagined… (= While I was lying…) |
| 이유 | because, as | **Feeling** confident, I had told everyone. (= Because I felt confident…) |
| 동시동작 | ~하면서, ~한 채 | **Holding** that card in my pocket, I opened the door. |
| 동시동작 | ~하면서 | **Standing** outside the classroom door, I could hear my own heartbeat. |

### 3. 과거분사·형용사로 시작하는 분사구문

Being으로 시작하는 분사구문에서는 Being을 보통 생략합니다. 그래서 과거분사나 형용사로 시작하는 분사구문이 생깁니다.

| 원래 형태 | 분사구문 |
|---|---|
| Because I was focused on explaining my idea, … | (Being) **Focused** on explaining my idea, I slowly forgot about myself. |
| Because I was unable to sleep, … | (Being) **Unable** to sleep, I finally got up. |

### 4. 분사구문 vs 동명사 주어 구별

| 문장 | -ing의 정체 | 판별 |
|---|---|---|
| **Walking home** that evening, I felt like a winner. | 분사구문 | 콤마 뒤에 주어 I + 동사가 따로 있음 |
| **Riding it** around the parking lot was the most satisfying moment. (14편) | 동명사 주어 | -ing 덩어리 바로 뒤에 동사 was |
