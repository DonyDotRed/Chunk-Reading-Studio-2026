## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> Much of what is valuable in a small business never appears in a manual. A repair technician may diagnose a fault by the sound of a motor; a shopkeeper may sense which customer needs time to decide and which prefers to be left alone. The philosopher Michael Polanyi described this kind of understanding as tacit knowledge, observing that people often know more than they are able to put into words. This essay uses the example of a small neighborhood repair shop to define tacit knowledge more precisely, to illustrate how it is acquired, and to consider why it matters for businesses that depend on skill and trust.
>
> Tacit knowledge can be defined, in contrast to explicit knowledge, as knowledge that is difficult to articulate, record, or transfer through written instructions. Explicit knowledge includes facts and procedures that can be captured in documents, such as the wiring diagram of a radio or the price list of a shop. Tacit knowledge, by comparison, is embedded in perception and practice. It includes the ability to recognize patterns, to judge when something is "not quite right," and to adjust one's actions to circumstances that no manual anticipated.
>
> My grandfather's repair shop offers a clear illustration. He kept a shelf of service manuals, yet he rarely consulted them. When a customer brought in a faulty clock, he would listen to it, hold it at different angles, and often identify the problem before opening the case. When I asked how he knew, his explanations were typically vague: the clock "sounded tired," or the hands "moved too politely." These descriptions were not mystical; they were compressed summaries of thousands of previous observations that he could no longer retrieve individually.
>
> How, then, is such knowledge transmitted? Because it resists written description, tacit knowledge is usually acquired through a gradual process of observation, imitation, and guided practice, a process traditionally associated with apprenticeship. In my own case, this process followed a recognizable sequence. Initially, I was permitted only to watch. Later, I was asked to perform small supporting tasks, such as holding a flashlight. Eventually, I was allowed to disassemble devices independently, while my grandfather asked questions rather than providing answers. Each stage increased my responsibility while preserving opportunities for correction.
>
> Tacit knowledge has significant implications for small businesses. On the one hand, it can constitute a genuine competitive advantage. A competitor may copy a shop's prices, its products, or even its appearance, but cannot easily replicate the judgment of an experienced practitioner. On the other hand, tacit knowledge creates vulnerability. When it resides entirely in one individual, it may disappear when that person retires or becomes unavailable. My grandfather's shop closed when he retired, in part because no one had acquired his skills in full.
>
> Tacit knowledge also plays a less obvious role in building trust. Customers often cannot evaluate technical expertise directly; instead, they infer it from behavior, such as the confidence of a diagnosis or the willingness to explain a fault in plain language. In this sense, tacit knowledge is not only a technical asset but a social one, since it shapes how customers perceive reliability.
>
> In conclusion, tacit knowledge refers to understanding that is acquired through experience and is difficult to express explicitly. As the repair shop illustrates, it is learned through observation and guided practice rather than through instruction alone, and it can serve both as a source of competitive advantage and as a point of fragility. Businesses that rely on such knowledge would therefore benefit from deliberately creating opportunities for apprenticeship, so that what cannot be written down can at least be passed on.

작은 가게에서 가치 있는 것의 상당 부분은 매뉴얼에 결코 나오지 않는다.
어휘: manual 매뉴얼, 설명서

**② A repair technician may diagnose a fault by the sound of a motor; a shopkeeper may sense which customer needs time to decide and which prefers to be left alone.**
수리 기술자는 모터 소리만으로 결함을 진단하기도 하고, 가게 주인은 어떤 손님에게 고를 시간이 필요하고 어떤 손님이 혼자 두기를 원하는지 알아채기도 한다.
어휘: technician 기술자 · diagnose 진단하다 · fault 결함 · shopkeeper 가게 주인 · sense 감지하다 · prefer 선호하다

**③ The philosopher Michael Polanyi described this kind of understanding as tacit knowledge, observing that people often know more than they are able to put into words.**
철학자 마이클 폴라니는 이런 이해를 암묵지라고 부르며, 사람들은 흔히 말로 표현할 수 있는 것보다 더 많이 알고 있다고 지적했다.
어휘: philosopher 철학자 · describe A as B A를 B라고 묘사하다 · tacit knowledge 암묵지 · observe 관찰하다, 말하다 · put into words 말로 표현하다

**④ This essay uses the example of a small neighborhood repair shop to define tacit knowledge more precisely, to illustrate how it is acquired, and to consider why it matters for businesses that depend on skill and trust.**
이 글은 동네의 작은 수리점을 예로 들어 암묵지를 더 정확히 정의하고, 그것이 어떻게 습득되는지 보여 주며, 기술과 신뢰에 기대는 가게에 왜 중요한지 고찰한다.
어휘: precisely 정확하게 · illustrate 예증하다 · acquire 습득하다 · depend on ~에 의존하다

### 문단 2

**⑤ Tacit knowledge can be defined, in contrast to explicit knowledge, as knowledge that is difficult to articulate, record, or transfer through written instructions.**
암묵지는 명시지와 대비하여, 글로 된 설명으로는 표현하거나 기록하거나 전달하기 어려운 지식으로 정의할 수 있다.
어휘: in contrast to ~와 대조적으로 · explicit knowledge 명시지 · articulate 명확히 표현하다 · transfer 전달하다 · instruction 지시

**⑥ Explicit knowledge includes facts and procedures that can be captured in documents, such as the wiring diagram of a radio or the price list of a shop.**
명시지에는 라디오의 배선도나 가게의 가격표처럼 문서에 담을 수 있는 사실과 절차가 포함된다.
어휘: procedure 절차 · capture 담다, 포착하다 · document 문서 · wiring diagram 배선도 · price list 가격표

**⑦ Tacit knowledge, by comparison, is embedded in perception and practice.**
그에 비해 암묵지는 지각과 실천 속에 녹아 있다.
어휘: by comparison 그에 비해 · embedded 내재된, 박혀 있는 · perception 지각

**⑧ It includes the ability to recognize patterns, to judge when something is "not quite right," and to adjust one's actions to circumstances that no manual anticipated.**
암묵지에는 패턴을 알아보는 능력, 무언가 "어딘가 이상하다"는 순간을 판단하는 능력, 그리고 어떤 매뉴얼도 예상하지 못한 상황에 맞춰 행동을 조정하는 능력이 포함된다.
어휘: ability 능력 · recognize 알아보다 · judge 판단하다 · adjust 조정하다 · circumstance 상황 · anticipate 예상하다

### 문단 3

**⑨ My grandfather's repair shop offers a clear illustration.**
할아버지의 수리점은 분명한 예를 보여 준다.
어휘: illustration 예시

**⑩ He kept a shelf of service manuals, yet he rarely consulted them.**
할아버지는 정비 매뉴얼을 선반 가득 두고 있었지만 좀처럼 펼쳐 보지 않았다.
어휘: shelf 선반 · service manual 정비 매뉴얼 · consult 참고하다

**⑪ When a customer brought in a faulty clock, he would listen to it, hold it at different angles, and often identify the problem before opening the case.**
손님이 고장 난 시계를 가져오면, 할아버지는 소리를 듣고 여러 각도로 들어 보다가 케이스를 열기도 전에 문제를 찾아내곤 했다.
어휘: faulty 결함 있는 · would (과거의 습관) · angle 각도 · case 케이스, 외함

**⑫ When I asked how he knew, his explanations were typically vague: the clock "sounded tired," or the hands "moved too politely."**
어떻게 알았느냐고 물으면 대답은 대개 모호했다. 시계가 "지친 소리를 낸다"거나, 바늘이 "너무 얌전하게 움직인다"는 식이었다.
어휘: explanation 설명 · typically 대개 · vague 모호한 · politely 얌전하게, 공손하게

**⑬ These descriptions were not mystical; they were compressed summaries of thousands of previous observations that he could no longer retrieve individually.**
이런 묘사는 신비한 것이 아니었다. 이제는 하나하나 떠올릴 수 없게 된 수천 번의 관찰을 압축한 요약이었다.
어휘: mystical 신비로운 · compressed 압축된 · summary 요약 · previous 이전의 · observation 관찰 · retrieve 되살리다, 회수하다 · individually 개별적으로

### 문단 4

**⑭ How, then, is such knowledge transmitted?**
그렇다면 이런 지식은 어떻게 전해지는가?
어휘: transmit 전달하다

**⑮ Because it resists written description, tacit knowledge is usually acquired through a gradual process of observation, imitation, and guided practice, a process traditionally associated with apprenticeship.**
암묵지는 글로 묘사하기 어렵기 때문에, 대개 관찰과 모방, 지도받는 실습이라는 점진적 과정을 통해 습득된다. 전통적으로 도제 제도와 연결되어 온 과정이다.
어휘: resist 저항하다, 잘 ~되지 않다 · gradual 점진적인 · imitation 모방 · guided 지도받는 · (동격) · be associated with ~와 연관되다 · apprenticeship 도제 제도

**⑯ In my own case, this process followed a recognizable sequence.**
내 경우 이 과정은 뚜렷한 순서를 따랐다.
어휘: recognizable 알아볼 수 있는 · sequence 순서

**⑰ Initially, I was permitted only to watch.**
처음에는 지켜보는 것만 허락되었다.
어휘: initially 처음에 · permit 허락하다

**⑱ Later, I was asked to perform small supporting tasks, such as holding a flashlight.**
그다음에는 손전등을 드는 것 같은 작은 보조 작업을 맡았다.
어휘: supporting 보조하는

**⑲ Eventually, I was allowed to disassemble devices independently, while my grandfather asked questions rather than providing answers.**
마침내는 혼자 기기를 분해해도 되었고, 할아버지는 답을 주기보다 질문을 던졌다.
어휘: eventually 결국 · disassemble 분해하다 · device 기기 · independently 독립적으로 · provide 제공하다

**⑳ Each stage increased my responsibility while preserving opportunities for correction.**
단계마다 책임은 늘어났지만, 바로잡을 기회는 여전히 남아 있었다.
어휘: stage 단계 · responsibility 책임 · preserve 보존하다, 유지하다 · correction 수정

### 문단 5

**㉑ Tacit knowledge has significant implications for small businesses.**
암묵지는 작은 가게에 중요한 함의를 지닌다.
어휘: significant 중요한 · implication 함의

**㉒ On the one hand, it can constitute a genuine competitive advantage.**
한편으로 암묵지는 진정한 경쟁 우위가 될 수 있다.
어휘: on the one hand 한편으로는 · constitute 구성하다, ~이 되다 · genuine 진정한 · competitive advantage 경쟁 우위

**㉓ A competitor may copy a shop's prices, its products, or even its appearance, but cannot easily replicate the judgment of an experienced practitioner.**
경쟁자는 가게의 가격이나 상품, 심지어 겉모습까지 베낄 수 있어도, 숙련된 장인의 판단력은 쉽게 복제할 수 없다.
어휘: competitor 경쟁자 · appearance 외관 · replicate 복제하다 · judgment 판단력 · experienced 숙련된 · practitioner 실무자

**㉔ On the other hand, tacit knowledge creates vulnerability.**
다른 한편으로 암묵지는 취약성을 낳는다.
어휘: vulnerability 취약성

**㉕ When it resides entirely in one individual, it may disappear when that person retires or becomes unavailable.**
암묵지가 오로지 한 사람 안에만 있으면, 그 사람이 은퇴하거나 자리를 비우는 순간 사라질 수 있다.
어휘: reside (특정한 곳에) 있다 · retire 은퇴하다 · unavailable 이용할 수 없는

**㉖ My grandfather's shop closed when he retired, in part because no one had acquired his skills in full.**
할아버지의 가게는 할아버지가 은퇴하면서 문을 닫았는데, 그 기술을 온전히 익힌 사람이 아무도 없었던 것도 한 이유였다.
어휘: in part 부분적으로 · in full 온전히

### 문단 6

**㉗ Tacit knowledge also plays a less obvious role in building trust.**
암묵지는 신뢰를 쌓는 데도 눈에 덜 띄는 역할을 한다.
어휘: play a role 역할을 하다 · obvious 명백한

**㉘ Customers often cannot evaluate technical expertise directly; instead, they infer it from behavior, such as the confidence of a diagnosis or the willingness to explain a fault in plain language.**
손님은 기술적 전문성을 직접 평가하기 어렵기 때문에, 진단의 확신이나 결함을 쉬운 말로 설명해 주려는 태도 같은 행동을 보고 그것을 추론한다.
어휘: evaluate 평가하다 · expertise 전문성 · infer 추론하다 · confidence 확신 · diagnosis 진단 · plain 쉬운, 평이한

**㉙ In this sense, tacit knowledge is not only a technical asset but a social one, since it shapes how customers perceive reliability.**
이런 의미에서 암묵지는 기술적 자산일 뿐 아니라 사회적 자산이기도 하다. 손님이 신뢰성을 어떻게 인식하는지를 좌우하기 때문이다.
어휘: asset 자산 · one (= asset) · shape 형성하다 · perceive 인식하다 · reliability 신뢰성

### 문단 7

**㉚ In conclusion, tacit knowledge refers to understanding that is acquired through experience and is difficult to express explicitly.**
결론적으로, 암묵지란 경험을 통해 습득되고 명시적으로 표현하기 어려운 이해를 가리킨다.
어휘: refer to ~을 가리키다 · explicitly 명시적으로

**㉛ As the repair shop illustrates, it is learned through observation and guided practice rather than through instruction alone, and it can serve both as a source of competitive advantage and as a point of fragility.**
수리점의 예가 보여 주듯, 암묵지는 지시만이 아니라 관찰과 지도받는 실습을 통해 배우며, 경쟁 우위의 원천이 될 수도 있고 약점이 될 수도 있다.
어휘: serve as ~의 역할을 하다 · source 원천 · fragility 취약함

**㉜ Businesses that rely on such knowledge would therefore benefit from deliberately creating opportunities for apprenticeship, so that what cannot be written down can at least be passed on.**
따라서 이런 지식에 기대는 가게라면 도제식 배움의 기회를 의도적으로 만들어, 글로 적을 수 없는 것을 적어도 전해 줄 수는 있도록 하는 것이 좋을 것이다.
어휘: benefit from ~으로부터 이득을 보다 · deliberately 의도적으로 · so that ~하도록 · pass on 전하다

## 학술 영어 포인트

### 1. 정의하기: 세 가지 문형

| 문형 | 구조 | 이 글의 예 |
|---|---|---|
| 형식적 정의 | X can be defined as + 상위 범주 + 구별 특징 | Tacit knowledge **can be defined**, in contrast to explicit knowledge, **as knowledge that** is difficult to articulate… |
| 대조 정의 | in contrast to Y / by comparison | **in contrast to** explicit knowledge / Tacit knowledge, **by comparison**, is embedded in… |
| 지시 정의 | X refers to ~ | tacit knowledge **refers to** understanding that is acquired through experience |
| 출처 기반 정의 | A described X as Y | Polanyi **described** this kind of understanding **as** tacit knowledge |

**형식적 정의의 공식**: 용어 + is defined as + **상위 범주**(knowledge) + **구별 특징**(that is difficult to articulate…). 상위 범주 없이 특징만 나열하면 정의가 불분명해집니다.
