## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> Good morning, graduates, families, and teachers. Twelve years ago, I sat exactly where you are sitting now. I was nervous, excited, and completely unsure about what came next. So I will not pretend to give you a map. Instead, I want to give you three small tools for the journey.
>
> The first tool is a question: what problem do you want to solve? Not what job you want, not what title you want, but what problem you want to solve. Jobs change. Titles change. Problems, however, are everywhere, waiting for someone who cares enough to notice them. If you look for problems instead of positions, you will never run out of work that matters.
>
> The second tool is a habit: start small, start now, and start again. When I left this school, I had a dozen big ideas and no plan for any of them. My first business failed in six months. My second failed in six weeks. My third is still running today. Was I smarter the third time? Not really. I was simply quicker to test, quicker to listen, and quicker to change.
>
> The third tool is a promise: be someone people can trust. In the years ahead, you will meet people who are more talented than you, more confident than you, and better connected than you. You cannot control that. What you can control is whether you keep your word, whether you treat others fairly, and whether you admit your mistakes. In the long run, those choices will matter more than any talent you were born with.
>
> Some of you already know exactly where you are going. Many of you do not. Let me tell you a secret: neither did most of the people you admire. They did not begin with certainty. They began with curiosity, with courage, and with a willingness to be wrong.
>
> So ask better questions. Take smaller steps. Keep bigger promises. Do not wait to feel ready, because readiness rarely arrives before action. It arrives because of it.
>
> Graduates, the world does not need you to be perfect. It needs you to be present, to be honest, and to be brave enough to begin. Congratulations, and thank you.

더 작은 걸음을 내딛으십시오.

**㉜ Keep bigger promises.**
더 큰 약속을 지키십시오.

**㉝ Do not wait to feel ready, because readiness rarely arrives before action.**
준비됐다고 느낄 때까지 기다리지 마십시오. 준비됨은 행동보다 먼저 오는 일이 드물기 때문입니다.
어휘: readiness 준비된 상태

**㉞ It arrives because of it.**
준비됨은 행동 덕분에 찾아옵니다.
어휘: (대조: before action ↔ because of it)

### 문단 7

**㉟ Graduates, the world does not need you to be perfect.**
졸업생 여러분, 세상은 여러분이 완벽하기를 바라지 않습니다.

**㊱ It needs you to be present, to be honest, and to be brave enough to begin.**
세상은 여러분이 그 자리에 있고, 정직하고, 시작할 만큼 용감하기를 바랍니다.
어휘: (병렬: to be ~ ×3) · present 그 자리에 있는

**㊲ Congratulations, and thank you.**
축하합니다. 감사합니다.

## 문법 포인트

### 1. 병렬 구조의 원칙

and, or, but으로 연결되는 요소는 **같은 품사, 같은 형태**여야 합니다. 이 원칙이 지켜지면 문장이 리듬감 있고 기억하기 쉬워집니다. 연설문이 병렬 구조를 집중적으로 쓰는 이유입니다.

| 병렬 요소 | 이 글의 예 |
|---|---|
| 형용사 × 3 | I was **nervous**, **excited**, and completely **unsure**. |
| 명령문 × 3 | **start small**, **start now**, and **start again** |
| 비교급 + to부정사 × 3 | **quicker to test**, **quicker to listen**, and **quicker to change** |
| 비교급 + than × 3 | **more talented** than you, **more confident** than you, and **better connected** than you |
| whether절 × 3 | **whether** you keep your word, **whether** you treat others fairly, and **whether** you admit your mistakes |
| 전치사구 × 3 | **with** curiosity, **with** courage, and **with** a willingness to be wrong |
| to부정사 × 3 | **to be** present, **to be** honest, and **to be** brave enough to begin |
| 명사절 (not A, not B, but C) | **Not what job** you want, **not what title** you want, **but what problem** you want to solve |

**병렬 오류 예시.**
- ❌ I was quicker to test, **listening**, and **I changed**.
- ✅ I was quicker **to test**, **to listen**, and **to change**.

### 2. 연설의 수사 장치

| 장치 | 설명 | 이 글의 예 |
|---|---|---|
| 3단 나열 (tricolon) | 세 항목을 묶어 리듬과 완결감을 줌 | 거의 모든 문단 |
| 두운 반복 (anaphora) | 문장 첫머리를 반복 | **My first** business… **My second**… **My third**… |
| 짧은 문장 반복 | 단순한 구조로 강조 | **Jobs change. Titles change.** |
| 대조 (antithesis) | 반대 개념을 나란히 | readiness rarely arrives **before action**. It arrives **because of it**. |
| 수사 의문문 | 답을 기대하지 않는 질문 | **Was I smarter the third time?** Not really. |
| 비교급 명령 | 짧고 강한 행동 지침 | Ask **better** questions. Take **smaller** steps. Keep **bigger** promises. |
