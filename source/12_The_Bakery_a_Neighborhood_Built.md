## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> On the corner of Maple Street, there is a small bakery called Morning Loaf. It was opened twelve years ago by a woman named Mrs. Han. At that time, the building was owned by the city, and it had been empty for almost five years. Most people thought that no business could survive there.
>
> Mrs. Han did not have much money. The ovens were bought from a restaurant that was closing, and the tables were made from old doors. Even the sign was painted by a local high school student. On the first day, only twelve loaves of bread were sold.
>
> Then something unusual happened. Mrs. Han asked her customers a simple question: "What kind of bread do you miss?" Their answers were written on a large board by the door. An elderly man asked for the rye bread that was baked in his hometown. A young mother wanted soft rolls that could be eaten by small children. A student asked for something cheap that could be carried to school.
>
> Each request was taken seriously. New recipes were tested every week, and the best ones were added to the menu. Some ideas failed, and they were quietly removed. Customers were invited to taste the new bread for free and give honest feedback. Slowly, the bakery was shaped by the people who used it.
>
> Today, Morning Loaf is known across the city. More than five hundred loaves are baked every morning, and the shop is often visited by tourists. Eight people are now employed there, and three of them were once regular customers. Last year, it was chosen as the best small business in the district. Yet the board by the door is still used. New requests are still written on it every day.
>
> When Mrs. Han is asked about her success, she always gives the same answer. "This bakery was not built by me," she says. "It was built by a neighborhood. I only listened."

## 문법 포인트

### 1. 수동태의 기본: be + 과거분사(p.p.)

주어가 동작을 "하는" 쪽이 아니라 "당하는" 쪽일 때 수동태를 씁니다. 동작을 한 사람보다 동작을 받은 대상이 더 중요할 때 자연스럽습니다.

| 능동태 | 수동태 |
|---|---|
| A woman **opened** the bakery. | The bakery **was opened** by a woman. |
| A student **painted** the sign. | The sign **was painted** by a student. |
| They **bake** 500 loaves every morning. | 500 loaves **are baked** every morning. |

### 2. 시제와 조동사에 따른 수동태

| 형태 | 구조 | 이 글의 예 |
|---|---|---|
| 현재 | am/is/are + p.p. | 500 loaves **are baked** every morning. |
| 과거 | was/were + p.p. | It **was opened** twelve years ago. |
| 조동사 | can/could + be + p.p. | rolls that **could be eaten** by small children |
| 5형식 수동 | be + p.p. + to부정사 | Customers **were invited to taste** the new bread. |
| 상태 수동 | be known / be employed | Morning Loaf **is known** across the city. |

### 3. by + 행위자를 생략하는 경우

이 글의 수동태 문장 중 절반 이상은 by가 없습니다. 행위자가 뻔하거나(빵은 빵집이 굽는다), 중요하지 않거나, 일반 사람일 때 by를 생략합니다.

- **By 있음**: It was opened **by a woman named Mrs. Han**. → 누가 열었는지가 핵심 정보
- **By 없음**: New recipes were tested every week. → 시험한 사람보다 시험했다는 사실이 중요

마지막 문단의 "It was built **by a neighborhood**"는 by를 일부러 남겨 "누가 만들었는가"를 강조하는 문장입니다. 글 전체의 결론이 by 뒤에 있습니다.

### 4. 헷갈리는 수동 표현

| 표현 | 의미 | 예 |
|---|---|---|
| be made from | (원재료 변형) ~로 만들어지다 | Bread is made from flour. |
| be made of | (재료 형태 유지) ~로 만들어지다 | The tables were made of old doors. |
| be known as | ~로 알려지다 (자격) | It is known as a friendly shop. |
| be known for | ~로 유명하다 (이유) | It is known for its rye bread. |

본문의 "made from old doors"는 문짝이 형태를 바꿔 테이블이 되었다는 느낌입니다. 문짝 모양이 그대로 남아 있다면 made of가 더 정확합니다.
