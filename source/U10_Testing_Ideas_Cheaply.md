## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> This report presents the results of a two-week classroom exercise in which a team of four students was given ten dollars and asked to generate as much profit as possible for a local animal shelter. During the first week, the team tested four business ideas at the smallest feasible scale before committing resources to any of them. The results are reported below in terms of cost, revenue, profit, and time, followed by a discussion of how the choice of performance measure influenced the team's decision.
>
> The four tests differed considerably in their financial outcomes. The cookie sale required three dollars in ingredients and generated ten dollars in revenue, yielding a profit of seven dollars. The car wash cost one dollar in soap and produced eight dollars from two customers, also yielding a profit of seven dollars. The phone case test was the least successful: a single case cost approximately five dollars to produce and sold for six, leaving a profit of roughly one dollar. Homework tutoring required no material costs and generated fifteen dollars from three younger students, making it the most profitable of the four tests in absolute terms.
>
> When the results were expressed relative to cost, a different ranking emerged. The car wash returned seven dollars for every dollar spent, compared with approximately 2.3 dollars per dollar for the cookie sale and 0.2 dollars per dollar for phone cases. On this measure, the car wash appeared to be the most efficient use of money. Tutoring could not be evaluated in this way at all, since its material cost was zero.
>
> A third ranking emerged when time was taken into account. The team recorded approximately four hours for the cookie sale, three hours for the car wash, two hours for the phone case, and three hours for tutoring. Profit per hour was therefore about 1.75 dollars for cookies, 2.33 dollars for the car wash, 0.50 dollars for phone cases, and 5.00 dollars for tutoring. By this measure, tutoring outperformed every alternative by a wide margin.
>
> These findings illustrate a general point about performance measures: the "best" option depends on which resource is most constrained. Because the team's money was limited but its time was relatively flexible, profit per dollar might initially seem the appropriate criterion. In practice, however, the binding constraint was time, since all four members had classes and other commitments. The team therefore prioritized profit per hour and, in the second week, combined tutoring with a smaller cookie operation, which could be prepared in the evenings and sold during tutoring sessions. By the end of the exercise, the initial ten dollars had grown to 112 dollars.
>
> Several limitations qualify these results. Each test was conducted once, with very small numbers of customers; the car wash, for example, was based on only two transactions. The figures should therefore be interpreted as rough indications rather than reliable estimates. In addition, time records were approximate, and the novelty of the exercise may have increased demand from classmates in ways that would not persist.
>
> Despite these limitations, the exercise demonstrates the value of testing ideas cheaply before committing to them. For a total outlay of nine dollars, the team obtained information that a single untested plan could not have provided. Perhaps more importantly, it learned that the same data can support different conclusions depending on the question being asked.

어휘: require 필요로 하다 · ingredient 재료 · yield (결과를) 내다

**⑥ The car wash cost one dollar in soap and produced eight dollars from two customers, also yielding a profit of seven dollars.**
세차는 세제에 1달러를 쓰고 손님 두 명에게서 8달러를 받아, 역시 7달러의 이익을 냈다.
어휘: soap 세제 · produce 만들어 내다

**⑦ The phone case test was the least successful: a single case cost approximately five dollars to produce and sold for six, leaving a profit of roughly one dollar.**
휴대폰 케이스 시험은 가장 성과가 낮았다. 케이스 하나를 만드는 데 약 5달러가 들었고 6달러에 팔려, 이익은 1달러 남짓이었다.
어휘: approximately 대략 · roughly 대략

**⑧ Homework tutoring required no material costs and generated fifteen dollars from three younger students, making it the most profitable of the four tests in absolute terms.**
숙제 지도는 재료비가 전혀 들지 않았고 후배 세 명에게서 15달러를 벌어, 절대 금액으로는 네 시험 중 가장 수익성이 높았다.
어휘: tutoring 개인 지도 · material cost 재료비 · profitable 수익성 있는 · in absolute terms 절대 금액 기준으로

### 문단 3

**⑨ When the results were expressed relative to cost, a different ranking emerged.**
결과를 비용 대비로 나타내자 다른 순위가 나타났다.
어휘: express 표현하다 · relative to ~에 비해 · ranking 순위 · emerge 나타나다

**⑩ The car wash returned seven dollars for every dollar spent, compared with approximately 2.3 dollars per dollar for the cookie sale and 0.2 dollars per dollar for phone cases.**
세차는 쓴 돈 1달러마다 7달러의 이익을 냈는데, 쿠키 판매는 1달러당 약 2.3달러, 휴대폰 케이스는 1달러당 0.2달러였다.
어휘: return (이익을) 내다 · compared with ~와 비교하여

**⑪ On this measure, the car wash appeared to be the most efficient use of money.**
이 기준으로는 세차가 돈을 가장 효율적으로 쓴 것으로 보였다.
어휘: measure 척도, 기준 · efficient 효율적인

**⑫ Tutoring could not be evaluated in this way at all, since its material cost was zero.**
지도는 재료비가 0이었기 때문에 이 방식으로는 아예 평가할 수 없었다.
어휘: evaluate 평가하다

### 문단 4

**⑬ A third ranking emerged when time was taken into account.**
시간을 고려하자 세 번째 순위가 나타났다.
어휘: take ~ into account ~을 고려하다

**⑭ The team recorded approximately four hours for the cookie sale, three hours for the car wash, two hours for the phone case, and three hours for tutoring.**
팀은 쿠키 판매에 약 4시간, 세차에 3시간, 휴대폰 케이스에 2시간, 지도에 3시간을 쓴 것으로 기록했다.
어휘: record 기록하다

**⑮ Profit per hour was therefore about 1.75 dollars for cookies, 2.33 dollars for the car wash, 0.50 dollars for phone cases, and 5.00 dollars for tutoring.**
따라서 시간당 이익은 쿠키가 약 1.75달러, 세차가 2.33달러, 휴대폰 케이스가 0.50달러, 지도가 5.00달러였다.
어휘: per ~당

**⑯ By this measure, tutoring outperformed every alternative by a wide margin.**
이 기준으로는 지도가 다른 모든 대안을 큰 차이로 앞섰다.
어휘: outperform 능가하다 · alternative 대안 · by a wide margin 큰 차이로

### 문단 5

**⑰ These findings illustrate a general point about performance measures: the "best" option depends on which resource is most constrained.**
이 결과는 성과 지표에 관한 일반적인 요점을 보여 준다. "최선"의 선택은 어떤 자원이 가장 부족한지에 달려 있다는 것이다.
어휘: illustrate 예증하다 · option 선택지 · constrained 제약된

**⑱ Because the team's money was limited but its time was relatively flexible, profit per dollar might initially seem the appropriate criterion.**
팀의 돈은 한정되어 있었지만 시간은 비교적 여유가 있었기 때문에, 처음에는 1달러당 이익이 적절한 기준으로 보일 수 있었다.
어휘: relatively 비교적 · flexible 유연한 · initially 처음에 · appropriate 적절한 · criterion 기준

**⑲ In practice, however, the binding constraint was time, since all four members had classes and other commitments.**
그러나 실제로 결과를 묶은 제약은 시간이었다. 네 명 모두 수업과 다른 일정이 있었기 때문이다.
어휘: in practice 실제로는 · binding constraint 실제로 결과를 묶는 제약 · commitment 해야 할 일, 약속

**⑳ The team therefore prioritized profit per hour and, in the second week, combined tutoring with a smaller cookie operation, which could be prepared in the evenings and sold during tutoring sessions.**
그래서 팀은 시간당 이익을 우선시했고, 둘째 주에는 지도에 소규모 쿠키 판매를 결합했다. 쿠키는 저녁에 준비해 지도 시간에 팔 수 있었다.
어휘: prioritize 우선시하다 · combine 결합하다 · operation 운영, 사업 · session 회차, 시간

**㉑ By the end of the exercise, the initial ten dollars had grown to 112 dollars.**
활동이 끝났을 때 처음의 10달러는 112달러로 늘어나 있었다.
어휘: initial 처음의

### 문단 6

**㉒ Several limitations qualify these results.**
이 결과에는 몇 가지 한계가 따른다.
어휘: limitation 한계 · qualify (주장에) 단서를 달다, 제한하다

**㉓ Each test was conducted once, with very small numbers of customers; the car wash, for example, was based on only two transactions.**
각 시험은 한 번씩만, 그것도 아주 적은 손님을 대상으로 이루어졌다. 예를 들어 세차 결과는 거래 두 건에 근거한 것이다.
어휘: conduct 수행하다 · transaction 거래

**㉔ The figures should therefore be interpreted as rough indications rather than reliable estimates.**
따라서 이 수치는 신뢰할 만한 추정치가 아니라 대략적인 경향으로 해석해야 한다.
어휘: figure 수치 · interpret 해석하다 · rough 대략적인 · indication 지표, 시사 · estimate 추정치

**㉕ In addition, time records were approximate, and the novelty of the exercise may have increased demand from classmates in ways that would not persist.**
또한 시간 기록은 어림값이었고, 활동이 새롭다는 점 때문에 반 친구들의 수요가 오래가지 않을 방식으로 일시적으로 늘었을 수도 있다.
어휘: approximate 대략적인 · novelty 새로움 · demand 수요 · persist 지속되다

### 문단 7

**㉖ Despite these limitations, the exercise demonstrates the value of testing ideas cheaply before committing to them.**
이런 한계에도 불구하고, 이 활동은 아이디어에 본격적으로 뛰어들기 전에 싸게 시험해 보는 일의 가치를 보여 준다.
어휘: demonstrate 입증하다, 보여 주다 · commit to ~에 전념하다

**㉗ For a total outlay of nine dollars, the team obtained information that a single untested plan could not have provided.**
팀은 총 9달러의 지출로, 시험해 보지 않은 단일 계획이었다면 결코 얻을 수 없었을 정보를 얻었다.
어휘: outlay 지출, 경비 · obtain 얻다 · untested 시험되지 않은 · could not have p.p. ~할 수 없었을 것이다

**㉘ Perhaps more importantly, it learned that the same data can support different conclusions depending on the question being asked.**
아마 더 중요한 것은, 같은 데이터라도 어떤 질문을 던지느냐에 따라 서로 다른 결론을 뒷받침할 수 있다는 사실을 배웠다는 점이다.

## 학술 영어 포인트

### 1. 수치 결과를 보고하는 동사와 구문

| 표현 | 기능 | 이 글의 예 |
|---|---|---|
| generate / produce | 수입·결과를 만들어 냄 | **generated** ten dollars in revenue / **produced** eight dollars |
| yield / leave | 결과로 남김 | **yielding** a profit of seven dollars / **leaving** a profit of roughly one dollar |
| return X for every dollar | 투입 대비 산출 | **returned** seven dollars **for every dollar spent** |
| compared with | 비교 수치 병기 | **compared with** approximately 2.3 dollars per dollar |
| outperform … by a wide margin | 큰 차이로 앞섬 | tutoring **outperformed** every alternative **by a wide margin** |
| in absolute terms / relative to | 절대값 / 상대값 | the most profitable **in absolute terms** / expressed **relative to** cost |

### 2. 순위가 바뀌는 지점을 표시하는 표현

| 표현 | 이 글의 예 |
|---|---|
| a different ranking emerged | When the results were expressed relative to cost, **a different ranking emerged**. |
| A third ranking emerged when… | **A third ranking emerged when** time was taken into account. |
| On this measure, … / By this measure, … | **On this measure**, the car wash appeared… / **By this measure**, tutoring outperformed… |

결과 절에서는 "어떤 지표로 보았을 때"인지를 문장 앞에 명시해야 독자가 수치를 오해하지 않습니다.

### 3. 결과에 단서를 다는 표현

| 표현 | 의미 | 이 글의 예 |
|---|---|---|
| qualify | (결과·주장에) 단서를 달다 | Several limitations **qualify** these results. |
| based on only two transactions | 표본 크기 명시 | the car wash… **was based on only two transactions** |
| rough indications rather than reliable estimates | 해석 수준 조정 | should be interpreted **as rough indications rather than reliable estimates** |
| the novelty… may have increased demand | 일시적 요인 가능성 | **may have increased** demand… in ways that would not persist |

### 4. 원작 10편과의 연결

| 10편 서사 | U10 결과 보고 |
|---|---|
| We spent three dollars on ingredients and sold twenty cookies for fifty cents each. | The cookie sale required three dollars in ingredients and generated ten dollars in revenue, yielding a profit of seven dollars. |
| It was the most profitable idea, but it was also the most tiring. | tutoring was the most profitable in absolute terms / profit per hour… 5.00 dollars |
| The best idea is not always the one you like most. It is the one that the numbers support. | the same data can support different conclusions depending on the question being asked |

10편의 결론("숫자가 뒷받침하는 아이디어")을 U10은 한 단계 더 밀고 나갑니다. **어떤 숫자를 보느냐**에 따라 뒷받침되는 아이디어가 달라진다는 것입니다.
