## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> My grandfather had a small repair shop near the train station. The room was narrow and dark, but it never felt small to me. The smell of oil and old wood made me feel safe. People called him "the Doctor of Broken Things."
>
> Every Saturday, my parents let me stay at the shop all day. At first, Grandfather only let me watch. He kept his tools clean and his table neat. "A messy table makes a messy mind," he often said.
>
> After a few months, he had me hold the flashlight while he worked. Later, he let me open old radios and clocks by myself. I found most machines difficult, but he never made me feel stupid. He simply asked, "What do you see?" and waited for my answer.
>
> His customers were usually not rich. Some brought him toasters that cost less than the repair. Once, a little boy brought a toy car with a broken wheel and only two coins. Grandfather fixed it and made the boy keep his coins. "Happy customers come back," he told me. "And they bring their friends."
>
> He was right. His shop never had a sign or an advertisement, but it was always busy. People trusted him because he kept his promises and kept his prices fair.
>
> My grandfather closed the shop when he turned eighty. Today the building is a coffee shop, but I still think of him whenever I pass by. He never called himself a businessman. Still, he taught me the most important rule of business: when you make people's lives a little easier, they will find you.

## 문법 포인트

### 1. 5형식: 주어 + 동사 + 목적어(O) + 목적격 보어(C)

목적격 보어는 목적어의 상태나 동작을 설명합니다. "O가 C하다/이다"의 관계가 성립하는지 확인하세요.

| 동사 | 구조 | 의미 | 이 글의 예 |
|---|---|---|---|
| make | make + O + 동사원형 | O가 ~하게 만들다 (사역) | The smell **made me feel** safe. |
| let | let + O + 동사원형 | O가 ~하도록 허락하다 | My parents **let me stay** at the shop. |
| have | have + O + 동사원형 | O에게 ~하게 시키다 | He **had me hold** the flashlight. |
| keep | keep + O + 형용사 | O를 ~한 상태로 유지하다 | He **kept his tools clean**. |
| find | find + O + 형용사 | O가 ~하다고 여기다 | I **found most machines difficult**. |
| call | call + O + 명사 | O를 ~라고 부르다 | People **called him "the Doctor…"** |
| make | make + O + 형용사 | O를 ~하게 만들다 | **make people's lives a little easier** |

**사역동사 주의점.** make, let, have 뒤의 목적격 보어 자리에는 to 없는 동사원형이 옵니다.

- ✅ He **made the boy keep** his coins.
- ❌ He made the boy ~~to keep~~ his coins.

### 2. 같은 동사, 다른 문형

| 문장 | 문형 | 해석 |
|---|---|---|
| A messy table **makes a messy mind**. | 3형식 (make + O) | 지저분한 생각을 **만든다** |
| The smell **made me feel safe**. | 5형식 (make + O + 동사원형) | 나를 편안하게 **느끼게 했다** |
| He **kept his promises**. | 3형식 (keep + O) | 약속을 **지켰다** |
| He **kept his prices fair**. | 5형식 (keep + O + 형용사) | 가격을 공정하게 **유지했다** |

목적어 뒤에 목적어를 설명하는 말이 하나 더 붙어 있으면 5형식입니다.
