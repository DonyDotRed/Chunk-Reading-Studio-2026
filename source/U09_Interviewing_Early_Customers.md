## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> This section describes the method used to explore the needs of potential users of a simple study-planning tool before any version of the tool was built. Because the aim was exploratory rather than confirmatory, a qualitative approach based on semi-structured interviews was adopted. The procedure is reported in sufficient detail to allow other student teams to adapt it.
>
> **Participants**
>
> Twenty secondary-school students aged between fifteen and eighteen were recruited from three classes at a single school. Participants were selected through convenience sampling: invitations were posted in class group chats, and the first twenty volunteers who met the inclusion criterion, namely that they regularly prepared for written examinations, were included. No incentives were offered. It should be noted that this sampling strategy limits the extent to which the findings can be generalized beyond the school in question.
>
> **Interview protocol**
>
> Each interview lasted approximately fifteen minutes and was conducted in person by one member of the team. A semi-structured guide containing three core questions was prepared in advance. Participants were asked to describe the most difficult part of their study routine, to recount the last occasion on which they had missed a planned study session, and to explain what, if anything, they currently used to organize their work. Follow-up questions were asked where answers were brief or ambiguous. The order of questions was kept constant across interviews in order to improve comparability.
>
> Particular care was taken to avoid leading questions. Interviewers did not describe the proposed tool until the final minute of the interview, and questions such as "Would you use an app that reminds you to study?" were deliberately excluded, since they invite agreement rather than information. Instead, questions focused on past behavior, which is generally considered a more reliable indicator than hypothetical statements about future behavior.
>
> **Pilot test**
>
> Before the main interviews, the guide was piloted with two students who were not included in the final sample. As a result of the pilot, one question about "study tools" was rephrased, since both pilot participants interpreted it as referring only to paid applications. Piloting of this kind is widely recommended, since ambiguities in wording are difficult to detect from the researcher's own perspective.
>
> **Data collection**
>
> With participants' consent, each interview was audio-recorded and subsequently transcribed. Interviewers also wrote brief field notes immediately after each session, recording impressions that might not be captured in a transcript, such as hesitation or emphasis. Participants were informed that they could withdraw at any time and that their names would not be included in any report. Recordings were stored on a password-protected device and deleted after transcription.
>
> **Data analysis**
>
> Transcripts were analyzed using a simplified form of thematic analysis. Two team members independently read all twenty transcripts and assigned short descriptive codes to relevant statements. The codes were then compared, disagreements were discussed until a consensus was reached, and related codes were grouped into broader themes. A theme was retained only if it appeared in at least five interviews, a threshold chosen to reduce the influence of isolated remarks. The three most frequent themes are reported in the following section.
>
> **Limitations of the method**
>
> Interviews conducted by fellow students may have encouraged openness, but they may also have introduced social desirability bias, since participants might have wished to appear organized in front of their peers. In addition, the small and homogeneous sample means that the resulting themes should be treated as hypotheses for further testing rather than as conclusions about students in general.

### 문단 1

**① This section describes the method used to explore the needs of potential users of a simple study-planning tool before any version of the tool was built.**
이 절은 학습 계획 도구의 어떤 버전도 만들기 전에, 잠재 사용자의 필요를 탐색하기 위해 사용한 방법을 기술한다.
어휘: section 절, 부분 · describe 기술하다 · method 방법 · explore 탐색하다 · potential 잠재적인

**② Because the aim was exploratory rather than confirmatory, a qualitative approach based on semi-structured interviews was adopted.**
목적이 가설 검증이 아니라 탐색이었기 때문에, 반구조화 인터뷰에 기반한 질적 접근법을 택했다.
어휘: aim 목적 · exploratory 탐색적인 · confirmatory 확증적인 · qualitative 질적인 · semi-structured 반구조화된 · adopt 채택하다

**③ The procedure is reported in sufficient detail to allow other student teams to adapt it.**
다른 학생 팀이 이 절차를 상황에 맞게 응용할 수 있도록 충분히 상세하게 보고한다.
어휘: procedure 절차 · sufficient 충분한 · detail 세부 · adapt 조정하다, 맞게 고치다

### 문단 2 · Participants

**④ Twenty secondary-school students aged between fifteen and eighteen were recruited from three classes at a single school.**
한 학교의 세 학급에서 15세부터 18세까지의 중등학교 학생 20명을 모집했다.
어휘: secondary-school 중등학교의 · aged ~세의 · recruit 모집하다

**⑤ Participants were selected through convenience sampling: invitations were posted in class group chats, and the first twenty volunteers who met the inclusion criterion, namely that they regularly prepared for written examinations, were included.**
참가자는 편의 표집으로 선정했다. 학급 단체 대화방에 모집 글을 올리고, 포함 기준(필기시험을 정기적으로 준비함)을 충족한 자원자 중 먼저 신청한 20명을 포함했다.
어휘: select 선정하다 · convenience sampling 편의 표집 · invitation 초대 · volunteer 자원자 · meet 충족하다 · inclusion criterion 포함 기준 · examination 시험

**⑥ No incentives were offered.**
보상은 제공하지 않았다.
어휘: incentive 보상, 유인책

**⑦ It should be noted that this sampling strategy limits the extent to which the findings can be generalized beyond the school in question.**
이 표집 전략 때문에 결과를 해당 학교 밖으로 일반화하는 데 한계가 있다는 점에 유의해야 한다.
어휘: note 주목하다 · strategy 전략 · extent 정도 · generalize 일반화하다 · in question 해당하는, 문제의

### 문단 3 · Interview protocol

**⑧ Each interview lasted approximately fifteen minutes and was conducted in person by one member of the team.**
인터뷰는 각각 약 15분간 팀원 한 명이 대면으로 진행했다.
어휘: last 지속되다 · approximately 대략 · conduct 수행하다 · in person 직접, 대면으로

**⑨ A semi-structured guide containing three core questions was prepared in advance.**
세 가지 핵심 질문을 담은 반구조화 인터뷰 안내서를 미리 준비했다.
어휘: guide 안내서 · contain 포함하다 · core 핵심의

**⑩ Participants were asked to describe the most difficult part of their study routine, to recount the last occasion on which they had missed a planned study session, and to explain what, if anything, they currently used to organize their work.**
참가자에게 공부 일과에서 가장 어려운 부분을 설명하고, 계획한 공부 시간을 놓친 가장 최근의 경우를 이야기하고, 현재 공부를 정리하는 데 쓰는 것이 있다면 무엇인지 설명해 달라고 요청했다.
어휘: routine 일과 · recount 자세히 이야기하다 · occasion 경우, 때 · session 시간, 회차 · if anything 만약 있다면 · currently 현재 · organize 정리하다

**⑪ Follow-up questions were asked where answers were brief or ambiguous.**
답변이 짧거나 모호하면 후속 질문을 했다.
어휘: follow-up 후속의 · where (= in cases where) · ambiguous 모호한

**⑫ The order of questions was kept constant across interviews in order to improve comparability.**
비교 가능성을 높이기 위해 질문 순서는 모든 인터뷰에서 똑같이 유지했다.
어휘: constant 일정한 · comparability 비교 가능성

### 문단 4

**⑬ Particular care was taken to avoid leading questions.**
유도 질문을 피하기 위해 특히 주의를 기울였다.
어휘: care 주의 · take care 주의하다 · leading question 유도 질문

**⑭ Interviewers did not describe the proposed tool until the final minute of the interview, and questions such as "Would you use an app that reminds you to study?" were deliberately excluded, since they invite agreement rather than information.**
면담자는 인터뷰 마지막 1분 전까지 제안하려는 도구를 설명하지 않았고, "공부하라고 알려 주는 앱을 쓰시겠어요?" 같은 질문은 정보보다 동의를 끌어내기 때문에 의도적으로 뺐다.
어휘: interviewer 면담자 · proposed 제안된 · remind 상기시키다 · deliberately 의도적으로 · exclude 제외하다 · invite 유도하다 · agreement 동의

**⑮ Instead, questions focused on past behavior, which is generally considered a more reliable indicator than hypothetical statements about future behavior.**
대신 질문은 과거 행동에 초점을 맞췄다. 과거 행동은 미래 행동에 관한 가정적 진술보다 더 신뢰할 만한 지표로 여겨지기 때문이다.
어휘: behavior 행동 · , which (계속적 용법) · indicator 지표 · hypothetical 가정적인 · statement 진술

### 문단 5 · Pilot test

**⑯ Before the main interviews, the guide was piloted with two students who were not included in the final sample.**
본 인터뷰에 앞서, 최종 표본에 포함되지 않은 학생 두 명을 대상으로 인터뷰 안내서를 예비 시험했다.
어휘: pilot 예비 시험하다

**⑰ As a result of the pilot, one question about "study tools" was rephrased, since both pilot participants interpreted it as referring only to paid applications.**
예비 시험 결과, "공부 도구"에 관한 질문 하나를 바꿔 표현했다. 두 참가자 모두 그 질문을 유료 앱만 가리키는 것으로 이해했기 때문이다.
어휘: rephrase 바꿔 말하다 · interpret 해석하다 · application 애플리케이션

**⑱ Piloting of this kind is widely recommended, since ambiguities in wording are difficult to detect from the researcher's own perspective.**
질문 표현의 모호함은 연구자 자신의 관점으로는 알아차리기 어렵기 때문에, 이런 예비 시험은 널리 권장된다.
어휘: ambiguity 모호함 · detect 발견하다 · perspective 관점

### 문단 6 · Data collection

**⑲ With participants' consent, each interview was audio-recorded and subsequently transcribed.**
참가자의 동의를 얻어 모든 인터뷰를 녹음하고 이후 전사했다.
어휘: consent 동의 · audio-record 음성 녹음하다 · subsequently 그 후에 · transcribe 전사하다

**⑳ Interviewers also wrote brief field notes immediately after each session, recording impressions that might not be captured in a transcript, such as hesitation or emphasis.**
면담자는 각 인터뷰 직후 간단한 현장 기록도 남겨, 머뭇거림이나 강조처럼 전사본에 담기지 않을 수 있는 인상을 적었다.
어휘: field notes 현장 기록 · impression 인상 · capture 포착하다 · transcript 전사본 · hesitation 머뭇거림 · emphasis 강조

**㉑ Participants were informed that they could withdraw at any time and that their names would not be included in any report.**
참가자에게는 언제든 참여를 철회할 수 있으며 어떤 보고서에도 이름이 실리지 않는다고 알렸다.
어휘: inform 알리다 · withdraw 철회하다, 그만두다

**㉒ Recordings were stored on a password-protected device and deleted after transcription.**
녹음 파일은 암호가 걸린 기기에 보관했다가 전사 후 삭제했다.
어휘: store 저장하다 · password-protected 암호로 보호된 · delete 삭제하다 · transcription 전사

### 문단 7 · Data analysis

**㉓ Transcripts were analyzed using a simplified form of thematic analysis.**
전사본은 주제 분석을 단순화한 방식으로 분석했다.
어휘: analyze 분석하다 · simplified 단순화된 · thematic analysis 주제 분석

**㉔ Two team members independently read all twenty transcripts and assigned short descriptive codes to relevant statements.**
팀원 두 명이 각자 20개 전사본을 모두 읽고, 관련 있는 진술에 짧은 기술 코드를 붙였다.
어휘: independently 독립적으로 · assign 부여하다 · descriptive 기술적인 · code 코드 · relevant 관련 있는

**㉕ The codes were then compared, disagreements were discussed until a consensus was reached, and related codes were grouped into broader themes.**
이후 코드를 비교해 의견이 다른 부분은 합의에 이를 때까지 논의했고, 관련 코드를 더 넓은 주제로 묶었다.
어휘: disagreement 불일치 · consensus 합의 · reach 이르다 · group 묶다 · theme 주제

**㉖ A theme was retained only if it appeared in at least five interviews, a threshold chosen to reduce the influence of isolated remarks.**
주제는 최소 5개 인터뷰에서 나타난 경우에만 남겼는데, 한두 사람의 발언이 미치는 영향을 줄이려고 정한 기준이다.
어휘: retain 유지하다 · threshold 기준치, 문턱 · influence 영향 · isolated 고립된 · remark 발언

**㉗ The three most frequent themes are reported in the following section.**
가장 자주 나타난 세 주제는 다음 절에서 보고한다.
어휘: frequent 빈번한 · following 다음의

### 문단 8 · Limitations of the method

**㉘ Interviews conducted by fellow students may have encouraged openness, but they may also have introduced social desirability bias, since participants might have wished to appear organized in front of their peers.**
동료 학생이 진행한 인터뷰는 솔직한 답변을 끌어냈을 수 있지만, 참가자가 또래 앞에서 체계적으로 보이고 싶어 했을 수 있으므로 사회적 바람직성 편향을 낳았을 수도 있다.
어휘: fellow 동료의 · openness 솔직함 · introduce 끌어들이다 · social desirability bias 사회적 바람직성 편향 · organized 체계적인 · peer 또래

**㉙ In addition, the small and homogeneous sample means that the resulting themes should be treated as hypotheses for further testing rather than as conclusions about students in general.**
또한 표본이 작고 동질적이므로, 여기서 도출된 주제는 학생 일반에 관한 결론이 아니라 추가 검증이 필요한 가설로 다루어야 한다.
어휘: homogeneous 동질적인 · sample 표본 · resulting 결과로 생긴 · hypothesis–hypotheses 가설 · further 추가의 · in general 일반

### 4. 원작 09편과의 연결

| 09편 안내문 (명령문) | U09 방법 절 (과거 수동태) |
|---|---|
| **Start with** people you can reach. | Participants **were selected** through convenience sampling |
| **Prepare** three questions. | A semi-structured guide containing three core questions **was prepared** in advance. |
| Ask "What is the hardest part of your morning?" instead of "Do you like my idea?" | questions such as "Would you use an app…?" **were deliberately excluded**, since they invite agreement rather than information |
| **Don't sell** yet. | Interviewers **did not describe** the proposed tool until the final minute |
| **Write everything down.** | each interview **was audio-recorded and subsequently transcribed** |
| If three people describe the same problem, you have found something worth solving. | A theme **was retained only if** it appeared in at least five interviews |
