## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> Every spring, our school parking area was full of abandoned bicycles. They had flat tires, rusted chains, and broken seats. Most of them belonged to students who had graduated and simply left them behind. The school usually sent them to a recycling center.
>
> My friend Tae-min saw them differently. "Those aren't broken bicycles," he said. "They're unused bicycles waiting for a second chance." He asked the principal for permission, and the principal agreed, as long as we worked only on weekends.
>
> We started with three bikes. The first one, covered in dust and leaves, took us a whole Saturday. We watched repair videos, borrowed tools from a neighbor, and replaced the worn-out parts with used parts bought online. By evening, the bicycle was shining. Riding it around the parking lot was the most satisfying moment of my year.
>
> Then we faced a surprising problem. Nobody wanted the repaired bikes, even at a low price. We were confused. Finally, a freshman explained the reason. "I need a bike," she said, "but I don't know if a used bike is safe."
>
> That comment changed everything. We created a simple checklist and attached a signed safety card to each bicycle. The card showed every part we had checked and the date of the inspection. We also offered free repairs for the first month. Within two weeks, all three bikes were sold.
>
> By the end of the school year, we had fixed and sold twenty-four bicycles. The money earned from the sales was used to buy better tools, and the rest was donated to the school library. More importantly, fewer bikes ended up in the recycling center.
>
> Looking back, I realize that the bikes were never the real product. What people were buying was trust. A repaired bicycle is just a machine. A trusted bicycle is something people are willing to pay for.

## 문법 포인트

### 1. 현재분사 vs 과거분사

분사는 동사를 형용사처럼 써서 명사를 꾸밉니다. 꾸밈을 받는 명사가 동작을 **하면** 현재분사, **당하면** 과거분사입니다.

| 분사 | 의미 | 이 글의 예 |
|---|---|---|
| 현재분사 (-ing) | 능동·진행 (~하는, ~하고 있는) | bicycles **waiting** for a second chance (자전거가 기다리고 있음) |
| 과거분사 (p.p.) | 수동·완료 (~된, ~당한) | **abandoned** bicycles (자전거가 버려짐), **repaired** bikes (자전거가 수리됨) |

**판별 연습.** "a **signed** safety card"는 카드가 서명을 하는 게 아니라 서명을 받은 것이므로 과거분사입니다. "a **trusted** bicycle"도 자전거가 신뢰를 받는 쪽입니다.

### 2. 앞에서 꾸미기 vs 뒤에서 꾸미기

| 위치 | 조건 | 이 글의 예 |
|---|---|---|
| 명사 앞 | 분사 한 단어 | **rusted** chains, **broken** seats, **unused** bicycles, **used** parts |
| 명사 뒤 | 분사 + 다른 말(구) | bicycles **waiting for a second chance**, parts **bought online**, the money **earned from the sales** |
| 콤마로 삽입 | 부가 설명 | The first one, **covered in dust and leaves**, took us a whole Saturday. |

뒤에서 꾸미는 분사는 "관계대명사 + be동사"가 생략된 것으로 볼 수 있습니다.

- parts **bought** online = parts **which were bought** online
- bicycles **waiting** for a second chance = bicycles **which are waiting** for a second chance

12편의 "a bakery **called** Morning Loaf", "a woman **named** Mrs. Han"도 같은 구조였습니다.

### 3. 감정을 나타내는 분사

| 현재분사 (감정을 일으키는) | 과거분사 (감정을 느끼는) |
|---|---|
| a **surprising** problem 놀라운 문제 | We were **surprised**. 우리는 놀랐다. |
| the most **satisfying** moment 만족스러운 순간 | I was **satisfied**. 나는 만족했다. |
| a **confusing** reason 헷갈리게 하는 이유 | We were **confused**. 우리는 혼란스러웠다. |

사물이 감정을 일으키면 -ing, 사람이 감정을 느끼면 -ed가 기본입니다.

### 4. fewer vs less

> **fewer** bikes ended up in the recycling center

fewer는 셀 수 있는 명사(bikes), less는 셀 수 없는 명사(money, time)에 씁니다.
