## 학습 방법

| 단계 | 할 일 | 권장 횟수 |
|---|---|---|
| 1회차 | 청크 단위 직독직해. 청크마다 끊어 소리 내어 읽고 바로 한국어 뜻을 떠올린다 | 3회 |
| 2회차 | 문장 단위 직독직해. 한 문장을 한 호흡으로 읽고 의미를 확인한다 | 3회 |
| 3회차 | 원문만 보고 처음부터 끝까지 낭독한다. 시간을 재서 기록한다 | 3회 이상 |

낭독 속도는 분당 단어 수(WPM)로 기록합니다. 281단어를 3분에 읽으면 약 94 WPM입니다. 회독이 거듭될수록 속도가 오르고 끊기는 지점이 줄어드는지 확인하세요.

## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> **Day 1**
>
> Today I made a promise to myself. For thirty days, I will do one small thing every day for my bookmark shop. I have wanted to sell my drawings for two years, but I have never actually started. Tonight I drew one bookmark. It took an hour, and it was not very good.
>
> **Day 7**
>
> I have drawn a bookmark every day this week. My lines have become cleaner, and I have learned to use brighter colors. Yesterday I showed my work to my sister. She smiled and asked for one.
>
> **Day 10**
>
> Today was hard. I had a math test, and I came home very tired. I did not want to draw at all. But I made a tiny bookmark with just one star on it. It took only ten minutes, but it still counts.
>
> **Day 14**
>
> Two weeks have passed. I have made twenty bookmarks, but I have not sold any yet. This morning I almost gave up. Then I remembered my promise, so I took a photo of my best design and posted it online.
>
> **Day 21**
>
> Something surprising has happened. Five people have asked about my bookmarks since Sunday. One girl ordered three of them last night. I wrapped them carefully and mailed them today. My hands were shaking when I wrote the address.
>
> **Day 30**
>
> I have finished my thirty days. I have sold eleven bookmarks so far and earned a little money. More importantly, I have learned something about myself. I used to wait for big moments, but big moments rarely came. Small steps have carried me further than any big plan. My sister has used her bookmark every day since Day 7. Tomorrow is Day 31, and I already know what I will do.

## 문법 포인트

### 1. 현재완료 vs 과거시제

| 구분 | 형태 | 핵심 의미 | 이 글의 예 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 현재완료 · 계속 | have p.p. + for/since | 과거부터 지금까지 이어짐 | I **have wanted** to sell my drawings for two years. |
| 현재완료 · 경험 | have never/ever p.p. | 지금까지 해 본 적 있음/없음 | I **have never** actually **started**. |
| 현재완료 · 완료 | have p.p. (+ yet/already/so far) | 지금 시점에 끝났거나 아직 안 끝남 | I **have not sold** any **yet**. |
| 현재완료 · 결과 | have p.p. | 과거 일의 결과가 지금 남아 있음 | My lines **have become** cleaner. |
| 과거시제 | -ed / 불규칙 | 이미 끝난 특정 시점의 일 | **Yesterday** I **showed** my work… |

**가장 중요한 규칙.** yesterday, last night, this morning(이미 지난 경우), ago처럼 끝난 시점을 가리키는 표현은 현재완료와 함께 쓰지 않습니다.

- ✅ One girl **ordered** three of them **last night**.
- ❌ One girl ~~has ordered~~ three of them last night.

### 2. 현재완료와 잘 어울리는 신호어

| 신호어 | 의미 | 예 |
|---|---|---|
| for + 기간 | ~동안 | for two years |
| since + 시점 | ~이후로 | since Sunday |
| yet | (부정·의문) 아직 | I have not sold any yet. |
| already | 이미 | I have already finished. |
| so far | 지금까지 | I have sold eleven so far. |
| never / ever | 한 번도 ~않다 / 지금껏 | I have never started. |

### 3. 미리보기: used to + 동사원형

> I **used to wait** for big moments.

"(예전에는) ~하곤 했다(지금은 아니다)"는 뜻입니다. 지금은 달라졌다는 대비가 들어 있어 성찰 글에서 자주 쓰입니다.
