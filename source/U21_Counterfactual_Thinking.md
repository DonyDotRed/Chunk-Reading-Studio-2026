## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> Few sentences are as familiar as those beginning "If only I had…". Reflecting on a student café that closed after two years, a former manager writes that asking for help sooner might have kept the café open. Psychologists refer to such thoughts as counterfactuals: mental simulations of alternatives to events that have already occurred. This essay examines what counterfactual thinking is, why it occurs, and under what conditions it is useful rather than merely painful.
>
> Counterfactuals can be classified according to their direction. Upward counterfactuals imagine how things could have been better ("If I had shared the sales figures, we might have survived"), whereas downward counterfactuals imagine how things could have been worse ("If my partner had not stayed late every Friday, we would have closed in the first month"). The two types tend to produce different emotional effects. Upward counterfactuals are associated with regret and dissatisfaction, while downward counterfactuals are associated with relief.
>
> A frequently cited study illustrates this contrast vividly. Researchers analyzing footage of Olympic medalists found that bronze medalists tended to appear happier than silver medalists. A plausible interpretation is that silver medalists compared their result with winning gold, an upward counterfactual, while bronze medalists compared theirs with receiving no medal at all, a downward one. Objective success, in other words, was filtered through the alternatives each athlete imagined. Later studies have suggested that athletes' prior expectations also shape their reactions, which indicates that counterfactual comparisons are not the only factor involved.
>
> Given that upward counterfactuals are unpleasant, why do people generate them so readily? One influential account, sometimes called the functional theory of counterfactual thinking, proposes that they serve a preparatory purpose. By identifying a specific action that might have produced a better outcome, an upward counterfactual implicitly suggests what to do differently next time. The former manager's realization that the numbers should have been shared, for example, appears to have shaped a later practice of informing the team immediately when a project begins to go wrong.
>
> This account implies that the usefulness of a counterfactual depends on its content. Counterfactuals that focus on controllable actions ("If I had asked for help") can generate concrete intentions for the future. Counterfactuals that focus on uncontrollable factors ("If the economy had been stronger") or on stable personal characteristics ("If I were a different kind of person") offer little guidance and may simply prolong distress. Similarly, counterfactual thinking is most likely to be useful when the situation is likely to recur; reflecting on an error in a one-time event may produce regret without any practical benefit.
>
> Grammatically, counterfactuals are expressed through conditional structures that mark events as contrary to fact: the past perfect in the if-clause combined with would have, might have, or could have in the main clause, or mixed forms that link a past condition to a present result. These structures allow speakers to hold an actual event and an imagined alternative in mind at the same time, which is precisely what makes counterfactual reasoning possible.
>
> In conclusion, counterfactual thinking is neither inherently harmful nor inherently beneficial. Its value depends on its direction, its focus, and the likelihood that similar situations will arise again. When it identifies a controllable action in a recurring situation, the painful question "What if I had…?" can become the practical question "What will I do next time?"

**③ Psychologists refer to such thoughts as counterfactuals: mental simulations of alternatives to events that have already occurred.**
심리학자들은 이런 생각을 반사실이라고 부른다. 이미 일어난 일의 대안을 머릿속에서 시뮬레이션하는 것이다.
어휘: refer to A as B A를 B라고 부르다 · counterfactual 반사실(적 사고) · simulation 시뮬레이션, 모의 · alternative 대안 · occur 일어나다

**④ This essay examines what counterfactual thinking is, why it occurs, and under what conditions it is useful rather than merely painful.**
이 글은 반사실적 사고가 무엇이고, 왜 일어나며, 어떤 조건에서 그저 괴롭기만 한 것이 아니라 쓸모가 있는지 살펴본다.
어휘: merely 단지

### 문단 2

**⑤ Counterfactuals can be classified according to their direction.**
반사실은 방향에 따라 분류할 수 있다.
어휘: classify 분류하다 · direction 방향

**⑥ Upward counterfactuals imagine how things could have been better ("If I had shared the sales figures, we might have survived"), whereas downward counterfactuals imagine how things could have been worse ("If my partner had not stayed late every Friday, we would have closed in the first month").**
상향 반사실은 일이 어떻게 더 나을 수 있었는지 상상하고("매출 수치를 공유했더라면 살아남았을지도 몰라"), 하향 반사실은 어떻게 더 나쁠 수 있었는지 상상한다("동업자가 금요일마다 늦게까지 남지 않았다면 첫 달에 문을 닫았을 거야").
어휘: upward 위쪽으로의 · sales figure 매출 수치 · downward 아래쪽으로의 · partner 동업자

**⑦ The two types tend to produce different emotional effects.**
두 유형은 서로 다른 정서적 효과를 낳는 경향이 있다.
어휘: emotional 정서적인

**⑧ Upward counterfactuals are associated with regret and dissatisfaction, while downward counterfactuals are associated with relief.**
상향 반사실은 후회와 불만족과, 하향 반사실은 안도와 연관된다.
어휘: be associated with ~와 연관되다 · regret 후회 · dissatisfaction 불만족 · relief 안도

### 문단 3

**⑨ A frequently cited study illustrates this contrast vividly.**
자주 인용되는 한 연구가 이 대조를 생생하게 보여 준다.
어휘: cite 인용하다 · illustrate 예증하다 · contrast 대조 · vividly 생생하게

**⑩ Researchers analyzing footage of Olympic medalists found that bronze medalists tended to appear happier than silver medalists.**
올림픽 메달리스트들의 영상을 분석한 연구자들은 동메달리스트가 은메달리스트보다 더 행복해 보이는 경향이 있다는 사실을 발견했다.
어휘: footage (촬영된) 영상 · medalist 메달리스트 · bronze 동 · silver 은

**⑪ A plausible interpretation is that silver medalists compared their result with winning gold, an upward counterfactual, while bronze medalists compared theirs with receiving no medal at all, a downward one.**
그럴듯한 해석은, 은메달리스트는 자기 결과를 금메달과 비교했고(상향 반사실), 동메달리스트는 메달을 아예 못 받는 경우와 비교했다(하향 반사실)는 것이다.
어휘: plausible 그럴듯한 · interpretation 해석 · theirs (= their result) · one (= counterfactual)

**⑫ Objective success, in other words, was filtered through the alternatives each athlete imagined.**
다시 말해 객관적인 성공은 각 선수가 떠올린 대안이라는 필터를 거쳐 경험되었다.
어휘: objective 객관적인 · filter 거르다 · athlete 운동선수

**⑬ Later studies have suggested that athletes' prior expectations also shape their reactions, which indicates that counterfactual comparisons are not the only factor involved.**
이후 연구들은 선수들의 사전 기대도 반응에 영향을 준다고 시사했는데, 이는 반사실적 비교가 유일한 요인은 아니라는 뜻이다.
어휘: prior 사전의 · expectation 기대 · reaction 반응 · , which (앞 내용 전체) · comparison 비교

### 문단 4

**⑭ Given that upward counterfactuals are unpleasant, why do people generate them so readily?**
상향 반사실이 불쾌하다면, 사람들은 왜 그렇게 쉽게 그것을 떠올리는 걸까?
어휘: given that ~을 고려하면 · unpleasant 불쾌한 · generate 만들어 내다 · readily 쉽게, 선뜻

**⑮ One influential account, sometimes called the functional theory of counterfactual thinking, proposes that they serve a preparatory purpose.**
영향력 있는 한 설명, 이른바 반사실적 사고의 기능 이론은 이런 생각이 다음을 준비하는 목적을 수행한다고 본다.
어휘: account 설명 · functional 기능적인 · propose 제안하다 · serve 기여하다, 수행하다 · preparatory 준비의

**⑯ By identifying a specific action that might have produced a better outcome, an upward counterfactual implicitly suggests what to do differently next time.**
더 나은 결과를 낳았을지도 모르는 구체적 행동을 짚어 냄으로써, 상향 반사실은 다음번에 무엇을 다르게 해야 할지 암묵적으로 알려 준다.
어휘: identify 찾아내다 · outcome 결과 · implicitly 암묵적으로

**⑰ The former manager's realization that the numbers should have been shared, for example, appears to have shaped a later practice of informing the team immediately when a project begins to go wrong.**
예를 들어 숫자를 공유했어야 했다는 전 운영자의 깨달음은, 이후 프로젝트가 삐걱대기 시작하면 곧바로 팀에 알리는 습관으로 이어진 것으로 보인다.
어휘: realization 깨달음 · shape 형성하다 · practice 습관, 관행 · inform 알리다 · go wrong 잘못되다

### 문단 5

**⑱ This account implies that the usefulness of a counterfactual depends on its content.**
이 설명에 따르면 반사실이 유용한지는 그 내용에 달려 있다.
어휘: imply 함의하다 · usefulness 유용성 · content 내용

**⑲ Counterfactuals that focus on controllable actions ("If I had asked for help") can generate concrete intentions for the future.**
"도움을 청했더라면"처럼 통제 가능한 행동에 초점을 맞춘 반사실은 미래를 위한 구체적인 의도를 만들어 낼 수 있다.
어휘: controllable 통제 가능한 · concrete 구체적인 · intention 의도

**⑳ Counterfactuals that focus on uncontrollable factors ("If the economy had been stronger") or on stable personal characteristics ("If I were a different kind of person") offer little guidance and may simply prolong distress.**
반면 "경제가 더 좋았더라면"처럼 통제할 수 없는 요인이나 "내가 다른 사람이었다면"처럼 잘 바뀌지 않는 개인 특성에 초점을 맞춘 반사실은 지침을 거의 주지 못하고 괴로움만 늘릴 수 있다.
어휘: uncontrollable 통제 불가능한 · factor 요인 · economy 경제 · stable 안정적인 · characteristic 특성 · guidance 지침 · prolong 연장하다 · distress 고통

**㉑ Similarly, counterfactual thinking is most likely to be useful when the situation is likely to recur; reflecting on an error in a one-time event may produce regret without any practical benefit.**
마찬가지로 반사실적 사고는 같은 상황이 다시 올 가능성이 있을 때 가장 쓸모 있다. 다시 오지 않을 일의 실수를 곱씹으면 실질적 이득 없이 후회만 남을 수 있다.
어휘: recur 재발하다 · one-time 일회성의 · benefit 이득

### 문단 6

**㉒ Grammatically, counterfactuals are expressed through conditional structures that mark events as contrary to fact: the past perfect in the if-clause combined with would have, might have, or could have in the main clause, or mixed forms that link a past condition to a present result.**
문법적으로 반사실은 사건을 사실과 반대되는 것으로 표시하는 조건 구조로 표현된다. if절의 과거완료와 주절의 would have, might have, could have를 결합하거나, 과거 조건을 현재 결과에 잇는 혼합 형태를 쓴다.
어휘: grammatically 문법적으로 · conditional 조건의 · structure 구조 · mark 표시하다 · contrary to ~와 반대되는 · main clause 주절 · link 연결하다 · condition 조건

**㉓ These structures allow speakers to hold an actual event and an imagined alternative in mind at the same time, which is precisely what makes counterfactual reasoning possible.**
이런 구조 덕분에 화자는 실제 사건과 상상한 대안을 동시에 머릿속에 둘 수 있으며, 바로 이것이 반사실적 추론을 가능하게 한다.
어휘: hold 붙들다 · actual 실제의 · , which (앞 내용 전체) · reasoning 추론

### 문단 7

**㉔ In conclusion, counterfactual thinking is neither inherently harmful nor inherently beneficial.**
결론적으로 반사실적 사고는 본질적으로 해롭지도 이롭지도 않다.
어휘: inherently 본질적으로 · beneficial 이로운

**㉕ Its value depends on its direction, its focus, and the likelihood that similar situations will arise again.**
그 가치는 방향, 초점, 그리고 비슷한 상황이 다시 올 가능성에 달려 있다.
어휘: focus 초점 · likelihood 가능성 · arise 일어나다

**㉖ When it identifies a controllable action in a recurring situation, the painful question "What if I had…?" can become the practical question "What will I do next time?"**
반복되는 상황에서 통제 가능한 행동을 짚어 낼 때, "그때 ~했더라면?"이라는 아픈 질문은 "다음엔 무엇을 할까?"라는 실천적 질문이 될 수 있다.
어휘: recurring 반복되는

### 2. 반사실의 분류 (방향 × 초점)

| | 통제 가능한 행동에 초점 | 통제 불가능한 요인·고정된 특성에 초점 |
|---|---|---|
| **상향** (더 나을 수 있었다) | 미래를 위한 구체적 의도 생성 → **가장 유용** | 지침 없이 고통만 연장 |
| **하향** (더 나쁠 수 있었다) | 안도 + 잘한 점 확인 | 안도 |

반복되는 상황인지 일회성 상황인지가 세 번째 조건입니다(recurring vs one-time).

### 3. 기능 이론을 설명하는 표현

| 표현 | 기능 | 이 글의 예 |
|---|---|---|
| Given that A, why B? | 역설 제기 | **Given that** upward counterfactuals are unpleasant, **why do people generate them so readily?** |
| One influential account… proposes that… serve a … purpose | 기능적 설명 도입 | **One influential account… proposes that they serve a preparatory purpose.** |
| implicitly suggests | 암묵적 기능 | an upward counterfactual **implicitly suggests** what to do differently next time |
| This account implies that… depends on… | 이론의 함의 | **This account implies that** the usefulness… **depends on** its content |

U08의 "The mechanism also helps explain an observation that might otherwise seem puzzling"처럼, 겉보기 역설(불쾌한데 왜 자주 하는가?)을 이론으로 풀어내는 구조입니다.

### 4. 원작 21편과의 연결

| 21편 편지 | U21 분석 |
|---|---|
| If I had shared the numbers with you, you would have noticed what I missed. | upward counterfactual focused on a **controllable action** |
| If you had not been there, the café would have closed in its first month… | **downward counterfactual** → relief, 감사 |
| When a project starts to go wrong, I tell the team immediately. | the functional theory: counterfactual → **preparatory** behavior change |
| Without that experience, I would not be the person I am today. | 반사실이 미래 행동을 형성한 사례 |
