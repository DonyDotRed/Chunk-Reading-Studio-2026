## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> For years, "someday" was my favorite word. Someday I would learn the guitar. Someday I would start a blog. Someday I would visit my aunt in Canada. I enjoyed talking about these plans, and I loved imagining them. However, I never actually did any of them.
>
> Last year, I finally understood my problem. Saying "someday" made me feel good without doing anything. It was like a promise that never had to be kept. So I decided to stop using the word.
>
> The first rule was simple. Whenever I wanted to say "someday," I had to name a day instead. "I will learn the guitar" became "I will practice the guitar for ten minutes on Tuesday." At first, I hated doing this. A real date felt heavy and serious.
>
> Then something interesting happened. Because I wrote the dates down, I remembered to do the tasks. I also stopped to think before I made new plans. If I could not give a plan a date, maybe I did not really want it.
>
> Of course, I did not succeed every time. I tried writing a blog post every week, but I gave up after a month. Then I tried to write one every two weeks, and that worked better. Failing at the first plan was not the end. It simply showed me what was realistic.
>
> Today, I can play five songs on the guitar. My blog has twelve posts, and I am planning to visit my aunt next summer. I still remember sitting in my room and dreaming about these things. The dreams were nice, but they were not enough.
>
> I have not completely given up dreaming. I just avoid using one word. Now when I hear myself say "someday," I smile and ask a different question: "Which day?"

## 문법 포인트

### 1. 목적어로 무엇을 취하는가

| 유형 | 동사 | 이 글의 예 |
|---|---|---|
| 동명사만 | enjoy, avoid, finish, give up, mind | I **enjoyed talking**. / I just **avoid using** one word. / I have not **given up dreaming**. |
| to부정사만 | want, decide, plan, hope, need | I **decided to stop**. / I am **planning to visit** my aunt. |
| 둘 다, 의미 비슷 | love, like, hate, start | I **loved imagining** them. / I **hated doing** this. |

**외우는 요령.** to부정사는 "앞으로 할 일"(want, plan, decide), 동명사는 "이미 하고 있거나 겪는 일"(enjoy, avoid, give up)과 잘 어울리는 경향이 있습니다.

### 2. 둘 다 오지만 의미가 달라지는 동사

| 동사 | + 동명사 | + to부정사 |
|---|---|---|
| stop | ~하는 것을 그만두다: **stop using** the word | ~하기 위해 멈추다: **stopped to think** |
| remember | (과거에) ~한 것을 기억하다: **remember sitting** in my room | (앞으로) ~할 것을 기억하다: **remembered to do** the tasks |
| try | 시험 삼아 ~해 보다: **tried writing** a post every week | ~하려고 애쓰다: **tried to write** one every two weeks |

이 글에는 세 동사 모두 두 가지 형태가 한 번씩 들어 있습니다. 낭독할 때 짝을 찾아 비교해 보세요.

### 3. 동명사 주어

> **Saying** "someday" made me feel good.
> **Failing** at the first plan was not the end.

동명사가 주어 자리에 오면 "~하는 것은"으로 해석하고, 동사는 단수 취급합니다.
