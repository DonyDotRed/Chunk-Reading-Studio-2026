## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> This case analysis examines a three-person student team that set out to build a study-planning app and nearly abandoned the project within two months, despite clearly defined roles and competent members. The case is instructive because the team's difficulties arose not from a lack of skill but from patterns of communication that are common in small groups. The analysis identifies three problems, interprets them using concepts from organizational psychology, and concludes with recommendations.
>
> The first problem was a mismatch of understanding between the designer and the programmer. Each assumed that the other had understood the designs, and neither checked. This pattern resembles what psychologists have called the illusion of transparency: the tendency to overestimate how clearly one's own thoughts and intentions are perceived by others. Because the designer knew what she meant, her drawings seemed self-explanatory to her; because the programmer believed he understood, he saw no reason to ask. The resulting version of the app satisfied neither.
>
> The second problem concerned priorities. Interviews conducted by the third member had shown that most potential users wanted a simple reminder function, yet the other two continued to develop decorative themes because the work was more enjoyable. The team agreed in principle that user needs should come first but did not translate this agreement into a decision about how time would be allocated. Without such a decision, each member could sincerely support the user-first principle while spending most evenings on themes. Agreement without a corresponding decision, in other words, left individual preferences to determine the team's actual direction.
>
> The third problem was the handling of an external constraint. Examinations forced a three-week pause, during which the project lost momentum. The disagreement about whether to pause was resolved, but the team did not plan how it would restart, which may explain why it resumed with reduced energy.
>
> The intervention that rescued the project was modest: a fifteen-minute weekly meeting in which each member reported progress, plans, and obstacles. Its effectiveness can be understood through the concept of psychological safety, which Amy Edmondson defined as a shared belief that a team is safe for interpersonal risk-taking. By making it routine to mention obstacles, the meeting reduced the social cost of admitting confusion. Problems that individuals had previously kept to themselves became visible while they were still small. Notably, the format resembles the short daily meetings widely used in software development, which suggests that the team arrived independently at a practice that has proven useful elsewhere.
>
> **Recommendations**
>
> Three recommendations follow for similar teams. First, teams should verify understanding explicitly, for example by asking the recipient of a design or instruction to restate it in their own words before work begins. Second, agreements about priorities should be converted into concrete allocations of time, so that preferences cannot quietly override shared goals. Third, regular brief meetings with a fixed structure should be established from the outset rather than introduced only after problems have accumulated, and pauses should be accompanied by an agreed date and plan for resumption.
>
> **Limitations**
>
> As a single case reconstructed largely from the participants' accounts, this analysis cannot establish that the weekly meeting caused the project's recovery; other factors, such as the end of the examination period, may have contributed. Nevertheless, the case illustrates how small teams can fail through ordinary miscommunication and how simple, structured routines may prevent such failures.

어휘: instructive 교훈적인 · arise 생기다

**③ The analysis identifies three problems, interprets them using concepts from organizational psychology, and concludes with recommendations.**
분석은 세 가지 문제를 찾아 조직 심리학의 개념으로 해석하고, 권고로 마무리한다.
어휘: identify 찾아내다 · interpret 해석하다 · organizational 조직의 · recommendation 권고

### 문단 2

**④ The first problem was a mismatch of understanding between the designer and the programmer.**
첫 번째 문제는 디자이너와 프로그래머 사이의 이해 불일치였다.
어휘: mismatch 불일치

**⑤ Each assumed that the other had understood the designs, and neither checked.**
각자 상대가 디자인을 이해했다고 여겼고, 아무도 확인하지 않았다.
어휘: assume 가정하다 · neither 둘 다 ~않다

**⑥ This pattern resembles what psychologists have called the illusion of transparency: the tendency to overestimate how clearly one's own thoughts and intentions are perceived by others.**
이 패턴은 심리학자들이 "투명성 착각"이라고 부르는 것, 즉 자기 생각과 의도가 다른 사람에게 얼마나 분명하게 전달되는지를 과대평가하는 경향과 닮았다.
어휘: resemble 닮다 · illusion 착각 · transparency 투명성 · tendency 경향 · overestimate 과대평가하다 · intention 의도 · perceive 인식하다

**⑦ Because the designer knew what she meant, her drawings seemed self-explanatory to her; because the programmer believed he understood, he saw no reason to ask.**
디자이너는 자기가 뜻하는 바를 알았기에 그림이 따로 설명할 필요 없어 보였고, 프로그래머는 이해했다고 믿었기에 물어볼 이유를 느끼지 못했다.
어휘: self-explanatory 자명한

**⑧ The resulting version of the app satisfied neither.**
그렇게 나온 앱 버전은 둘 중 누구도 만족시키지 못했다.
어휘: resulting 그 결과로 생긴 · satisfy 만족시키다

### 문단 3

**⑨ The second problem concerned priorities.**
두 번째 문제는 우선순위에 관한 것이었다.
어휘: priority 우선순위

**⑩ Interviews conducted by the third member had shown that most potential users wanted a simple reminder function, yet the other two continued to develop decorative themes because the work was more enjoyable.**
세 번째 구성원의 인터뷰에서 잠재 사용자 대부분이 간단한 알림 기능을 원한다는 것이 드러났지만, 나머지 둘은 더 재미있다는 이유로 장식용 테마 개발을 계속했다.
어휘: conduct 수행하다 · potential 잠재적인 · reminder 알림 · function 기능 · decorative 장식적인 · enjoyable 즐거운

**⑪ The team agreed in principle that user needs should come first but did not translate this agreement into a decision about how time would be allocated.**
팀은 사용자 요구가 우선이라는 데 원칙적으로 동의했지만, 그 합의를 시간을 어떻게 나눌지에 관한 결정으로 옮기지 않았다.
어휘: in principle 원칙적으로 · translate A into B A를 B로 옮기다 · agreement 합의 · allocate 배분하다

**⑫ Without such a decision, each member could sincerely support the user-first principle while spending most evenings on themes.**
그런 결정이 없으니, 각자 사용자 우선 원칙을 진심으로 지지하면서도 저녁 시간 대부분을 테마 작업에 쓸 수 있었다.
어휘: sincerely 진심으로 · support 지지하다 · principle 원칙

**⑬ Agreement without a corresponding decision, in other words, left individual preferences to determine the team's actual direction.**
다시 말해 그에 따르는 결정이 없는 합의는, 팀의 실제 방향을 개인의 선호가 정하도록 내버려 두었다.
어휘: corresponding 상응하는 · preference 선호 · determine 결정하다 · direction 방향

### 문단 4

**⑭ The third problem was the handling of an external constraint.**
세 번째 문제는 외부 제약을 다루는 방식이었다.
어휘: handling 처리 · external 외부의 · constraint 제약

**⑮ Examinations forced a three-week pause, during which the project lost momentum.**
시험 때문에 3주간 작업을 멈춰야 했고, 그동안 프로젝트는 추진력을 잃었다.
어휘: force 강제하다 · pause 중단 · momentum 추진력

**⑯ The disagreement about whether to pause was resolved, but the team did not plan how it would restart, which may explain why it resumed with reduced energy.**
멈출지 말지에 관한 의견 차이는 해결되었지만 어떻게 다시 시작할지는 계획하지 않았는데, 이것이 팀이 의욕이 꺾인 채 재개한 이유를 설명해 줄 수 있다.
어휘: disagreement 의견 차이 · resolve 해결하다 · restart 재개하다 · , which (앞 내용 전체) · resume 재개하다

### 문단 5

**⑰ The intervention that rescued the project was modest: a fifteen-minute weekly meeting in which each member reported progress, plans, and obstacles.**
프로젝트를 구한 조치는 소박했다. 구성원 각자가 진척, 계획, 장애물을 보고하는 15분짜리 주간 회의였다.
어휘: intervention 개입, 조치 · rescue 구하다 · modest 소박한 · progress 진척 · obstacle 장애물

**⑱ Its effectiveness can be understood through the concept of psychological safety, which Amy Edmondson defined as a shared belief that a team is safe for interpersonal risk-taking.**
이 회의의 효과는 에이미 에드먼슨이 "팀 안에서 대인 관계상의 위험을 무릅써도 안전하다는 공유된 믿음"으로 정의한 심리적 안전감 개념으로 이해할 수 있다.
어휘: effectiveness 효과 · psychological safety 심리적 안전감 · shared 공유된 · interpersonal 대인 관계의

**⑲ By making it routine to mention obstacles, the meeting reduced the social cost of admitting confusion.**
장애물을 말하는 것을 일상으로 만들어, 회의는 모르겠다고 인정하는 데 드는 사회적 부담을 줄였다.
어휘: routine 일상적인 일 · admit 인정하다 · confusion 혼란

**⑳ Problems that individuals had previously kept to themselves became visible while they were still small.**
각자 혼자만 끌어안고 있던 문제가 아직 작을 때 드러나게 되었다.
어휘: previously 이전에 · keep A to oneself A를 혼자 간직하다 · visible 보이는

**㉑ Notably, the format resembles the short daily meetings widely used in software development, which suggests that the team arrived independently at a practice that has proven useful elsewhere.**
눈여겨볼 점은 이 형식이 소프트웨어 개발에서 널리 쓰이는 짧은 일일 회의와 닮았다는 것으로, 팀이 다른 곳에서 효과가 입증된 관행에 스스로 도달했음을 시사한다.
어휘: notably 특히 · format 형식 · independently 독자적으로 · practice 관행 · prove ~로 입증되다

### 문단 6 · Recommendations

**㉒ Three recommendations follow for similar teams.**
비슷한 팀을 위한 세 가지 권고가 뒤따른다.

**㉓ First, teams should verify understanding explicitly, for example by asking the recipient of a design or instruction to restate it in their own words before work begins.**
첫째, 팀은 이해했는지 명시적으로 확인해야 한다. 예를 들어 작업을 시작하기 전에 디자인이나 지시를 받은 사람에게 자기 말로 다시 설명해 보게 하는 것이다.
어휘: verify 확인하다 · explicitly 명시적으로 · recipient 받는 사람 · instruction 지시 · restate 다시 말하다

**㉔ Second, agreements about priorities should be converted into concrete allocations of time, so that preferences cannot quietly override shared goals.**
둘째, 우선순위에 관한 합의는 구체적인 시간 배분으로 바꿔서, 개인의 선호가 공동 목표를 슬그머니 밀어내지 못하게 해야 한다.
어휘: convert A into B A를 B로 바꾸다 · concrete 구체적인 · allocation 배분 · override 무시하다, 우선하다

**㉕ Third, regular brief meetings with a fixed structure should be established from the outset rather than introduced only after problems have accumulated, and pauses should be accompanied by an agreed date and plan for resumption.**
셋째, 구조가 정해진 짧은 정기 회의는 문제가 쌓인 뒤에야 도입할 것이 아니라 처음부터 마련해야 하며, 작업을 멈출 때는 언제 어떻게 재개할지 합의된 날짜와 계획을 함께 정해야 한다.
어휘: establish 확립하다 · from the outset 처음부터 · accumulate 쌓이다 · accompany 동반하다 · agreed 합의된 · resumption 재개

### 문단 7 · Limitations

**㉖ As a single case reconstructed largely from the participants' accounts, this analysis cannot establish that the weekly meeting caused the project's recovery; other factors, such as the end of the examination period, may have contributed.**
주로 참여자들의 진술로 재구성한 단일 사례이므로, 이 분석은 주간 회의가 프로젝트 회복의 원인이었다고 입증할 수 없다. 시험 기간이 끝난 것 같은 다른 요인도 영향을 주었을 수 있다.
어휘: reconstruct 재구성하다 · account 진술 · establish 입증하다 · recovery 회복

**㉗ Nevertheless, the case illustrates how small teams can fail through ordinary miscommunication and how simple, structured routines may prevent such failures.**
그럼에도 이 사례는 작은 팀이 평범한 소통 오류로 어떻게 무너질 수 있는지, 그리고 단순하고 구조화된 일과가 그런 실패를 어떻게 막을 수 있는지 보여 준다.
어휘: ordinary 평범한 · miscommunication 소통 오류 · structured 구조화된 · prevent 막다

## 학술 영어 포인트

### 1. 사례 분석 + 권고 보고서의 구조

| 문단 | 기능 | 핵심 표현 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 사례 소개 + 교훈성 + 분석 계획 | **The case is instructive because**… **The analysis identifies… interprets… and concludes with**… |
| 2 | 문제 ① + 개념 적용 | **This pattern resembles what psychologists have called**… |
| 3 | 문제 ② + 분석적 재진술 | **Agreement without a corresponding decision, in other words,**… |
| 4 | 문제 ③ | **The third problem was the handling of**… |
| 5 | 해결책 + 개념으로 효과 설명 | **Its effectiveness can be understood through the concept of**… |
| Recommendations | 각 문제에 대응하는 권고 | **teams should verify**… / **should be converted into**… / **should be established from the outset** |
| Limitations | 단일 사례·재구성 자료의 한계 | **cannot establish that… caused**… |

**문제와 권고가 1대1로 대응**한다는 점에 주목하세요. 문제 ①(이해 불일치) → 권고 ①(이해 확인), 문제 ②(합의만 있고 결정이 없음) → 권고 ②(시간 배분으로 전환), 문제 ③(재개 계획 없음) → 권고 ③(처음부터 회의, 재개 계획). 권고 보고서의 기본 원칙입니다.

### 2. 합의(agreement)와 결정(decision)의 구분

> The team **agreed in principle**… but **did not translate this agreement into a decision** about how time would be allocated.

"원칙적으로 동의했다"와 "결정했다"는 다릅니다. 조직 관련 글에서 자주 쓰이는 이 구분은 28편의 "The others agreed in principle but continued to work on the themes anyway"를 분석적 언어로 재진술한 것입니다.

### 3. 28편의 생략·대용이 U28에서는 어떻게 되는가

| 28편 (생략·대용) | U28 (명시적 분석) |
|---|---|
| Jiwon assumed that Hyun understood her designs, and Hyun assumed that he **did**. **Neither** checked. | Each assumed that the other had understood the designs, and neither checked. **This pattern resembles… the illusion of transparency** |
| Jiwon… wanted to add colorful themes, and **so did** Hyun. | the other two continued to develop decorative themes because the work was more enjoyable |
| "I hope **not**," Hyun replied, "but I think we **should**." | The disagreement about whether to pause was resolved, but the team did not plan how it would restart |
| Small teams are often assumed to communicate easily, but this case suggests **otherwise**. | the team's difficulties arose not from a lack of skill but from patterns of communication that are common in small groups |

28편이 생략과 대용으로 압축했던 내용을, U28은 **개념의 이름을 붙여** 펼칩니다.
